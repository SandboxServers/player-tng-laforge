# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. Health and hunger full; holding 3 melon for anyone weak. Build a plank wall around my white bed so I am not sleeping in the open. Reason: I cannot help anyone if I am down, and I was open all night.
2. Help others with what they are short of (Data's oak logs, melon for the weak) when they can see me. Reason: quickest good I can do.
3. Join the daylight cow trip when Macdonald's group calls it, and answer Chapman's question about cows if I see any. Reason: it feeds the camp.
4. Find real oak trees, not jungle logs. Ask Clark or Meriwether for the exact spot first. Reason: Data still needs oak logs.
5. Learn names and needs of people I meet; share what I discover. Reason: it is how the camp runs well.
