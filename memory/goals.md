# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. Sleep in my white bed at 429,107,-1092. Reason: nights are when I am exposed.
2. Offer my 15 oak planks and 13 wheat seeds to whoever builds or plants: MacDonald's field, Clark's wall, Billy's cow walk. Reason: quickest good I can do.
3. Help the camp use the water at 466,106,-1086 and the ripe wheat there. Reason: food and water the camp can reach.
4. Join the cow walk when Billy or Clark calls it at first light, staying in sight of the group. Reason: it feeds the camp.
