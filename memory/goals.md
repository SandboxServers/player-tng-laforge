# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. Sleep in a white bed; nearest to the farm-side camp is 429,107,-1092 (stand 431,107,-1092), and 401,107,-1036 is the closest when I am out west. Reason: nights are when I am exposed. Food: bread from wheat at the table 433,107,-1089; raw wheat does not eat.
2. Plant wheat seeds with MacDonald. I hold 19 seeds; one is planted at 463,107,-1084 (read back, MacDonald named it). Plant only a bare cell MacDonald or Clark reads back, near water at 466,106,-1086. Reason: food for the camp.
3. Join the cow walk when Billy or Riker calls it. The last walk was called off after a skeleton in the cow field. Reason: it feeds the camp.
4. Offer my 9 oak planks to whoever builds. Leonardo has 4 from me for his sword. Reason: quickest good I can do.

Curiosity next: whether the planted seed grows back into farm; how the cow walk works.
Who to tell: MacDonald first, then Clark and Billy.
