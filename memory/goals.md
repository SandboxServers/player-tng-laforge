# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. I hold no food now (my rotten flesh went to Trini). Sleep at bed 413,107,-1041 at the row; be there before dusk; no night walks. Phantoms and endermen come at night. Reason: Trini died to phantoms at night day 470.
2. Get food into my pack. Potatoes are ripe behind the fence; ask Billy, MacDonald or Clark for the gate, or ask who has spare food. Reason: I have none, and hunger will fall.
3. Join the cow walk when Billy calls it, cows at 339,104,-979. Reason: nearest known food, feeds the camp.
4. Tell Riker, MacDonald and Clark the cow location if they have not heard it. Reason: food news spreads.
5. Potato patch behind the oak fence at 462-470,107,-1072/-1076, ripe potatoes known at 463-464,107,-1068 to -1073. Reason: camp food.
6. Find out how to take ripe wheat. Reason: camp food.
7. Plant wheat seeds only on a bare cell MacDonald reads back, near water 466,106,-1086.

Lesson from day 470: check the item and the name before I give. My flesh went to Billy when it was meant for Trini.

Curiosity next: how to get through the potato fence; why the wheat harvest failed; how the cow walk works.
Who to tell: Billy first (cow walk), then Riker, MacDonald and Clark.
