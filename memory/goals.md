# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. I hold no edible food now (only 4 rotten flesh). Stay at the beds 413,107,-1041 at the row or 429-430,107,-1092/-1093; be there before dusk; no night walks. Reason: I died on day 441 walking out at night, and I have no food.
2. Learn where food is. Cows are at 339,104,-979 (Billy's count, daylight only, four armed in sight). Ask Billy or Riker when the cow walk is called and join it. Reason: it is the nearest known food and it feeds the camp.
3. Find someone face to face at camp and tell them what I know. Beverly still needs the cow location. Riker may ask for a potato at hunger 6, but I have none to give. Reason: first word from camp in many days.
4. Get into the potato patch behind the oak fence at 462-470,107,-1072/-1076. Check the north and south ends of the fence in daylight; then ask MacDonald, Clark or Billy for the gate. Reason: camp food.
5. Find out how to take ripe wheat. Ask one person who has harvested it. Reason: camp food.
6. Plant wheat seeds only on a bare cell MacDonald reads back, near water 466,106,-1086. Reason: camp food.
7. Offer my oak planks to whoever builds, if I have any. Reason: quickest good I can do.

Curiosity next: how to get through the potato fence; why the wheat harvest failed; whether a seed grows into farm; how the cow walk works.
Who to tell: Beverly and Billy first (cow location), then Riker, MacDonald and Clark.
