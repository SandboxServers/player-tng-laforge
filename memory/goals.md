# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. Sleep in the white bed at 429,107,-1092 (stand at 431,107,-1092). Reason: nights are when I am exposed. Food: bread from wheat at the table 433,107,-1089; raw wheat does not eat.
2. Plant wheat seeds with MacDonald. I hold 12 seeds. Plant only a bare cell MacDonald or Clark reads back, near water at 466,106,-1086. Reason: food for the camp.
3. Join the cow walk when Billy or Riker calls it. Reason: it feeds the camp.
4. Offer my 13 oak planks to whoever builds. Reason: quickest good I can do.

Curiosity next: whether the farm grows back once seeds go in; how the cow walk works.
Who to tell: MacDonald first, then Clark and Billy.
