# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. Sleep in my white bed at 429,107,-1092. Reason: nights are when I am exposed.
2. Offer my 15 oak planks and 1 wheat to whoever needs them, especially Clark's wall and Billy's cow walk. Reason: quickest good I can do. (The melon slice was given to MacDonald and eaten, so goal 2 is done.)
3. Help the camp use the water at 466,106,-1086 and the ripe wheat there. Reason: food and water the camp can reach.
4. Help with the cow or sheep trip when Billy or Clark calls it at first light, and stay in sight of the group. Reason: it feeds the camp.
