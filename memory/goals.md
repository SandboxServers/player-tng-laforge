# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. Stay at the beds 429-430,107,-1092/-1093; be there before dusk; no night walks. Reason: I died on day 441 walking out at night.
2. Find someone face to face at camp. Beverly spoke on day 466 and is at the row 414,-1041 to be in Billy's sight. Walk there in the morning, in clear weather, with food to share. Reason: first word from camp in many days.
3. Get into the potato patch behind the oak fence at 462-470,107,-1072/-1076. Check the north and south ends of the fence in daylight; then ask MacDonald, Clark or Billy for the gate. Reason: camp food.
4. Find out how to take ripe wheat. Ask one person who has harvested it. Reason: camp food.
5. Plant wheat seeds only on a bare cell MacDonald reads back, near water 466,106,-1086. Reason: camp food.
6. Join the cow walk when Billy or Riker calls it. Reason: it feeds the camp.
7. Offer my oak planks to whoever builds, if I have any. Reason: quickest good I can do.

Curiosity next: how to get through the potato fence; why the wheat harvest failed; whether a seed grows into farm; how the cow walk works.
Who to tell: Beverly and Billy first, then MacDonald and Clark.
