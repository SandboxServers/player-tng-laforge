# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. Sleep in my white bed at 429,107,-1092. Reason: nights are when I am exposed.
2. Give the melon slice to whoever drops to hunger 6 first (MacDonald or Clark). Reason: it is the one food I can give now.
3. Offer my 15 oak planks to anyone who needs them, especially Clark's wall and Billy's cow walk. Reason: quickest good I can do.
4. Help find the water, and tell the camp about the ripe wheat at 462,107,-1088. Reason: food the camp can reach.
5. Help with the sheep or cow trip when Billy calls it at first light. Reason: it feeds the camp.
