# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. I hold no edible food (only 4 rotten flesh) and no weapon. Sleep at bed 413,107,-1041 at the row or 429-430,107,-1092/-1093; be there before dusk; no night walks. Reason: I died on day 441 walking out at night, and I have no food.
2. Get a weapon, so I can count as one of the four armed for the cow walk. Wooden sword or spear from wood; ask Billy or Trini for one if I have no materials. Reason: the cow walk waits on four armed, and I am unarmed.
3. Learn where food is. Cows are at 339,104,-979 (Billy's count, daylight only, four armed in sight). Join the cow walk when Billy calls it at first light. Reason: nearest known food, and it feeds the camp.
4. Keep camp informed: Leonardo ate a potato today; tell Riker, MacDonald and Clark the cow location if they have not heard it. Reason: first word from camp in many days, and it spreads food news.
5. Get into the potato patch behind the oak fence at 462-470,107,-1072/-1076. Check the north and south ends of the fence in daylight; then ask MacDonald, Clark or Billy for the gate. Reason: camp food.
6. Find out how to take ripe wheat. Ask one person who has harvested it. Reason: camp food.
7. Plant wheat seeds only on a bare cell MacDonald reads back, near water 466,106,-1086. Reason: camp food.
8. Offer my oak planks to whoever builds, if I have any. Reason: quickest good I can do.

Curiosity next: how to get through the potato fence; why the wheat harvest failed; how the cow walk works.
Who to tell: Billy (weapon and walk), then Riker, MacDonald and Clark.
