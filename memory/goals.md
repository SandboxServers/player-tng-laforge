1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. Sleep in the white bed at 429,107,-1092 (I stand at 431,107,-1092). Reason: nights are when I am exposed.
2. Plant wheat seeds with MacDonald. I hold 12 seeds. Plant only a cell that MacDonald or Clark reads back as bare farmland with clear air above, within reach of the water at 466,106,-1086. Reason: food for the camp.
3. Join the cow walk when Billy or Riker calls it, staying in sight of the group. Reason: it feeds the camp.
4. Offer my 13 oak planks to whoever builds. Reason: quickest good I can do.

Curiosity next: whether the farm grows back once seeds go in, and how the cow walk works.
Who to tell: MacDonald first (he plants), then Clark and Billy.
