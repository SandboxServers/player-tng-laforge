# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. Health and hunger are full; holding 3 melon for anyone weak. Reason: I cannot help anyone if I am down.
2. Help others with what they are short of (melon for Leonardo and Tommy, logs, planks) when they can see me. Reason: I have food to spare and it is the quickest good I can do.
3. Join the daylight cow trip with Macdonald's group when it is called; food for everyone. Reason: it feeds the camp, but it has not been called for days.
4. Explore with care and write down what I learn. Reason: I have stayed near the beds too long; a short trip to the oak trees would teach me more.
5. Learn names and needs of people I meet; share what I discover.
