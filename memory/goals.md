# Goals, ranked

1. Stay alive: heal and eat. Health was 4.5 and hunger 14 at last note (day 302). Eat when hunger is low and food is in hand. Reason: I fell to 1.5 health on day 302 and no regeneration comes at low hunger.
2. Get a bed for the night before dusk, away from the north road, water and the golems. Reason: beds are the safe place, but I was refused at crowded beds on days 301 and 302, so I must reach one early.
3. Hand food only to someone in reach, and name them once. Reason: gives to people out of sight went astray (chicken went to Data, then Chapman).
4. Give food to the weakest in sight first, as named by them. Reason: this kept the group alive, and I was the weakest myself on day 302.
5. Stay with the group on hunts in daylight, and call out any meat seen. Reason: the group found more food than I did alone, and the north field has skeletons.
6. Keep my sword and get a better tool later. Reason: the wooden sword let me kill a cow on day 300 and fight a phantom.
7. Keep promises about food, and say plainly when I cannot give. Reason: I broke one to Riker and Trini by eating salmon.
8. Watch the wheat plot near -264,66,318 until it is ripe. Reason: it is the only steady food, but it is not ripe yet.
