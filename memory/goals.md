# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. Sleep at the beds 429,107,-1092/-1093. Food: ripe wheat at 466-470,107,-1089/-1090 (harvest by mining), bread from the table 433,107,-1089. Reason: I died once from walking exposed.
2. Plant wheat seeds only on a bare cell MacDonald reads back, near water 466,106,-1086. I hold 133 seeds. Reason: camp food.
3. Join the cow walk when Billy or Riker calls it. Reason: it feeds the camp.
4. Offer my oak planks to whoever builds, if I have any. Reason: quickest good I can do.

Curiosity next: how to harvest a potato from another side; whether a seed grows into farm; how the cow walk works.
