4. Find real oak trees, not jungle or birch logs. Ask Lewis, Troi or Data for the exact spot. Reason: Data still needs oak logs.

6. Learn names and needs of people I meet. Clark's 20 planks are collected and thanked; keep learning who needs what. Reason: it is how the camp runs well.
