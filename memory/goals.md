# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself fed, sheltered and safe. Sleep in a white bed, nearest 415,107,-1092 or 429,107,-1092. Food: bread from wheat at the table 433,107,-1089; raw wheat does not eat. Reason: I died once already from walking exposed.

4. Offer my 9 oak planks to whoever builds. Reason: quickest good I can do.
