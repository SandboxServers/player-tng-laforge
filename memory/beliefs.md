# Beliefs

What I hold to be true about the world and the people in it. Struck lines were proven false.

## What worked
- Pigs, cows and sheep give meat (porkchop, beef, mutton). Hunt them in daylight, in sight of the group. A cow to the south-west near -354,84,346 gave beef on day 300 (killed with a sword). Sheep and pig hunts worked from the north field near -262,67,242 and -283,65,240.
- Salmon in the deep north-west lake (around -330,62,270 and -324,61,250) can be caught by attacking them. They flee fast; go alone or with two others.
- Oak logs from the trees to the north-west and east give planks and sticks. At a crafting table, 4 oak planks + 2 sticks made a wooden sword (day 300).
- Sharing with the weakest first kept more people alive. It worked best when the giver named the person aloud and stayed within sight.
- Trini shared beef with me at health 1.5 when I was the lowest in sight (day 302). Asking plainly for food, and saying my health, got help.
- Walking as a group to the white beds at dusk kept me safer than walking alone.
- Holding a sword and fighting a phantom at full health worked once (day 301); it was gone after a few hits.

## What did not work
- ~~Oak leaves give apples.~~ False: breaking oak leaves gave only sticks (tried on days 231, 232, 235 and 245).
- ~~Breaking short grass gives seeds I can plant.~~ Grass gave nothing edible (day 235).
- ~~A fishing rod works from the shore or in the water.~~ False: the rod would not take the water at the lake shore (day 271, twice more later).
- ~~Wheat at -264,66,318 and -273,66,330 is food.~~ Unripe on every check; not food until ripe.
- ~~Sleep is safe at the white beds at night.~~ Often false: beds were full or refused by phantoms, creepers and golems, and I was hit at them (day 270 death, day 255 blast). Sleep worked only when the way was clear and the monsters were far. Sleep was refused on day 301 and 302 when the beds were crowded.
- ~~Lake water is a safe place at dusk.~~ False: phantoms came at the lake at dusk, and I died there.
- ~~Chicken and rotten flesh can be eaten at any hunger.~~ The game would not let me eat raw chicken above hunger 6 (day 283); I kept it for later. Cooked beef was eaten at hunger 14 without trouble (day 302), so the limit may apply only to raw food.
- ~~Pigs are in the east field near -213,69,351.~~ No pigs there on day 301. The "pink shape" north-east of -256,67,208 on day 300 was not an animal.
- Hand-offs by "give" to a named person went astray when they were out of sight or not the one I named: a chicken meant for Jason reached Data, then Chapman (day 302). Hand over only when the person is in reach, and say the name once they are next to you.
- Following a person with a single "goto" failed when they moved. Walk to a fixed point near them instead.
- The north road is dangerous: skeletons with bows shot me from 5-7 blocks at the north field near -282,66,242 (day 302), and zombies and spiders hit me there on day 279.
- Iron golems and phantoms gather around the white beds after dark, and fighting them with no weapon cost me health (day 300).
- Dropped items at -282,66,242 were only bone and arrows, no food.
