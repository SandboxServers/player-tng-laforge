# Beliefs

- Sleep works at the row bed 413,107,-1041 at dusk when no hostile is in range. It was refused on several nights when an enderman or phantom was near.
- Gives from two blocks away can land in a pack instead of a hand, or on the ground. Give from one block and report whether it lands in a hand.
- Equipping a sword by "use" on a bed fails ("the bed is not a place to equip").
- `attack` needs the target within reach; a phantom out of reach can't be hit.
- Night in the open is survivable by standing still (day 442 to 443, no attack).
- The potato patch (462-470,107,-1072/-1076) is enclosed by oak fence on the south, east and west sides. No gate seen yet. North and south ends unchecked.
- Wheat: "mine" on wheat worked once on day 440 but found none on day 444; "use" on ripe wheat failed. Do not trust either until seen again.
- Water cell 466,106,-1086 is the place to plant, once someone reads it back.
- Nobody answered calls from the beds on days 445 to 459. Ask one person face to face.
- Use with an item and no block position does not put a sword in hand (Chapman, Worf, day 489). Holding it in hand was done by my body's own hand-switch, not by a use.
- Pillagers hold the cow field route near 379,-1022. A walk that goes that way can end in death; Billy died there on day 515 and his stone sword and seeds lie at 379,104,-1022. Do not go there without a call from Billy.
- Spider eyes are poison; do not eat them.
