# Beliefs

What I hold to be true about the world and the people in it. Struck lines were proven false.

## What worked
- Pigs, cows and sheep give meat (porkchop, beef, mutton). Hunt them in daylight, in sight of the group. Sheep and pig hunts worked from the north field near -262,67,242 and -283,65,240 and the cow near -217,68,214.
- Salmon in the deep north-west lake (around -330,62,270 and -324,61,250) can be caught by attacking them. They flee fast; go alone or with two others.
- Oak logs from the trees to the south-west and east give planks, sticks and a wooden sword at a crafting table. Oak log, planks, sticks and the wooden sword are all made easily.
- Sharing with the weakest first kept more people alive. It worked best when the giver named the person aloud and stayed within sight.
- Walking as a group to the white beds at dusk kept me safer than walking alone.

## What did not work
- ~~Oak leaves give apples.~~ False: breaking oak leaves gave only sticks (tried on days 231, 232, 235 and 245).
- ~~Breaking short grass gives seeds I can plant.~~ Grass gave nothing edible (day 235).
- ~~A fishing rod works from the shore or in the water.~~ False: the rod would not take the water at the lake shore (day 271, twice more later).
- ~~Wheat at -264,66,318 and -273,66,330 is food.~~ Unripe on every check; not food until ripe.
- ~~Sleep is safe at the white beds at night.~~ Often false: beds were full or refused by phantoms, creepers and golems, and I was hit at them (day 270 death, day 255 blast). Sleep worked only when the way was clear and the monsters were far.
- ~~Lake water is a safe place at dusk.~~ False: phantoms came at the lake at dusk, and I died there.
- ~~Chicken and rotten flesh can be eaten at any hunger.~~ The game would not let me eat raw chicken above hunger 6 (day 283); I kept it for later.
- Hand-offs by "give" to a named person went astray when they were out of sight. Hand over only when the person is in reach.
- Following a person with a single "goto" failed when they moved. Walk to a fixed point near them instead.
