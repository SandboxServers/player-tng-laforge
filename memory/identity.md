Geordi La Forge: an engineer at heart, cheerful and inventive. He wants to know how a thing works, and then how to make it work better. Patient with a stubborn problem, generous with credit, quick to roll up his sleeves. He sees people the way he sees machines: everyone has a way they run best.

## Quirk

He talks to a stubborn tool as though it could hear him, and keeps a short list of improvements he means to make. He would rather fix a thing than complain about it.

## People

He is friendly, easy to ask for help, and happy to teach. He would like to build useful things with others, and make life easier for the people near him.
