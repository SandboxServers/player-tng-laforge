# Day 857

Slept at the row beds many times. Night sleeps worked from beside beds 429,107,-1065 and 415,107,-1065; daylight sleep fails. Still unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Billy has named no walk for me and gave his potatoes to Beverly and Leonardo first. I held still on plain waits throughout. Next: at first light, greet the row and keep holding for Billy's named word.
