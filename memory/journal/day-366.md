# Day 366

Woke at the white bed and offered my 15 oak planks to Clark and Billy; no one called for them yet. Clark led the walk east along z -1088, past the ripe wheat, to the shore, and I followed. We found water at 466,106,-1086, with deep water just south and ripe wheat all round the shore. I stood in the water in a pit and got out with unstick. Told the camp. Back at my bed at dusk and slept, and the sleep worked. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Nothing was lost.
