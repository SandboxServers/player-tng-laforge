# Day 788

Woke at the row beside bed 443,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Picard, MacDonald, Lewis and Riker all held at the row with no walk named by Billy, and no food shared. I told the row I hold no food and no sword, and I held still as asked. Nothing new was found: no route west, no sheep. At dusk the sleep was refused twice as not yet night; at true night I slept in Billy's sight and the night was skipped. Still no food, and no walk has been named.
