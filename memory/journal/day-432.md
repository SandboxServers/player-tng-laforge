# Day 432

Quiet day near the beds at 429-430,107,-1092. No one came for the cow walk or the planting read-back, so I did not plant. I held 4 bread, 2 raw wheat and 124 seeds the whole time. Health and hunger stayed at 20. At night I went to the bed at 429,107,-1092, slept a short while, then slept fully and woke safe. Nothing new to learn today beyond keeping safe and waiting for a call.
