# Day 470 (night)

Woke at the row with no food in my pack. Night came early; an enderman stood 7 blocks off, so sleep at the bed failed at first. I walked to bed 413,107,-1041 and slept after a short wait; 9 of 10 needed were asleep. Nothing new was learned about the potato fence or the cow walk today. Next morning: stay at the row, listen for Billy's count, ask for the potato gate.
