# Day 470

Night at the row. Billy would not call the cow walk; only a few were armed. I got a phantom off my back at the row, and Trini died to phantoms and respawned. My four rotten flesh went to Billy by mistake, not to Trini; Billy passed them on and she ate them. Sleep was blocked several times by an enderman and phantoms until the night quieted, and then I slept on my bed at 413,107,-1041 in Billy's sight. Lesson: when I give food, check the name is exactly the one I mean before I send it.
