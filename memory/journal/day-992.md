# Day 992

Held at the row bed 416,107,-1065 all day in sight of Billy, Worf, Jason, Zack, Tommy, Riker and Clark. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Worf found a gap to the north-west but named no route; Billy and Jason said they would walk only when he did. Nothing was walked, no food came in, no sword. Slept at the row bed at dusk; woke with nothing changed.
