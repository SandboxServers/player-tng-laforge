# Day 463

Quiet day. Checked the north and south ends of the potato fence in daylight on day 460's morning walk: no gate seen on either end. Stayed at the beds, called for camp, got no answer. Went to bed at dusk at the beds, safe, health 20, hunger 20. Holding 6 potatoes, 25 wheat, about 177 wheat seeds. No one from camp has answered for many days.
