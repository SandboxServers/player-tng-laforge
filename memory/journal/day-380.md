# Day 380

Quiet day. Woke at the bed with full health and hunger. Clark (William) said he had named no farmland cell and sent me to MacDonald. MacDonald said he would till one fresh grass block at the wheat field near 455,107,-1083 and name it before planting. I went there at midday but he was not in sight, and he had not named the block by dusk. I went back to my bed at 430,107,-1092 and slept. Nobody called the cow walk. I still hold 15 oak planks and no seeds, and nobody has asked for planks yet.
