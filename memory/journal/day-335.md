# Day 335

Quiet day at the bed row. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Lewis does not know where the real oak trees are, so Goal 4 is still open. Data, Bcrusher and Worf went east for sheep, turned back at dusk, and promised a first-light trip. I held melon for anyone weak and did not need to give any. I slept at my white bed at 429,107,-1093 at dusk on day 335.
