# Day 429

Quiet day. Stayed alone near the bed row, then walked to the wheat field at 466,107,-1085 by the water at midday to look. Ten ripe wheat stand there, and ripe potatoes and wheat lie to the west at 462-463. I left them standing since my hunger stayed at 20, and offered my 3 bread by voice. No planting read-back came from MacDonald or Clark, so the 114 seeds stay in my pack. Walked back to the bed at 430,107,-1093 and slept at night, with no harm and no one hurt.
