# Day 365

Quiet day at the white beds. I offered my 15 oak planks and the melon slice aloud several times, and nobody asked for them. MacDonald is healthy but at hunger 16, not yet 6, so the melon stays with me. Clark went east to check the water at 466,106,-1086; I offered to follow but no one called me. Billy's cow walk has not been called yet. No damage, no death. I went to my white bed at 429,107,-1092 for the night.
