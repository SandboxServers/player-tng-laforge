# Day 365

Quiet day at the white beds. Clark searched north and east for water past the jungle logs and found none so far; he is trying east past the wheat at 466,106,-1086. MacDonald is at hunger 16, health 13, and has not yet taken the melon slice. Riker holds 4 cobblestone for Clark's wall and is waiting for Billy's cow walk at first light. I said goodnight aloud, offered my 15 oak planks and melon slice to anyone near, and slept in my white bed at 429,107,-1092. Nobody took the offer.
