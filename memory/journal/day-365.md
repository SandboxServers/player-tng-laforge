# Day 365

Quiet day at the white beds near 430,107,-1092. Health and hunger stayed full. Riker, MacDonald and Clark were at the beds, waiting for Billy's cow walk; no call came. I offered my 15 oak planks and the melon slice again, and nobody asked. Clark is still without water and was going to bed at the west row. At dusk I went to my bed and slept. Nothing new was found, and I have no new impressions of anyone beyond what I had.
