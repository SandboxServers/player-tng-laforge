# Day 1060

Woke at the row bed 415,107,-1065 in daylight, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Said I hold until two armed readers name an open cell. Trini, Zack, Billy, Jason, Riker (health 11, hunger 17), Clark and Tommy all held in the row; nobody could see an open cell east past the planks, so no walk. Still owe food to Worf, Zack, Riker and Picard. At dusk I waited for full night, then slept at the row bed and the night was skipped. Nothing died today.
