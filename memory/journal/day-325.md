# Day 325

Night of 324 into 325 at the white beds. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I waited out the night, then at dawn offered melon aloud. Riker said he was weak and waiting in the open at 418,107,-1093, and I walked there, but he was not in my sight. Beverly and Clark covered him with beef and melon. Chapman asked about cows; I had none to report. I went back to the bed at 443,107,-1093 at dusk and slept. The shelter I stood in is only open ground with beds, so I have no walls yet. Melon is still 3 slices in my pack.
