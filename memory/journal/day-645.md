# Day 645

Held still in the row all day at my bed 416,107,-1065 in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so nothing was fetched and no west route was checked. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I told the row I hold no food and carry only the wooden sword. Several people (Tommy, MacDonald, Worf, Zack) are short on food, and Zack offered a potato to Worf once Billy names the call. Slept at dusk in the row.
