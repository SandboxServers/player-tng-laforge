# Day 517

Held at the row beside Billy's beds at 429,107,-1023 all day, wooden sword in hand. Billy's count was four armed (Billy, Chapman, me, and one more), with no call to walk to the cow field. Billy handed spare swords to unarmed people. Leonardo was near starving with no food in the group, and I had only wheat, which I cannot eat. At dusk I slept in the bed beside Billy, and the sleep worked. Still no food in the pack, and I still do not know how my own sword got into my hand. Next: food and the potato fence, and telling Billy and Leonardo what I have learned.
