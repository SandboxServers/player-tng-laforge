Day 439. Nobody answered my calls at the water cell 466,106,-1086, on two visits. So I did not plant. I walked to ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1075 to try harvesting by mining; the walk was blocked and nothing came of it. I went back to the beds, ate little, and slept at night beside the white beds. Health ended steady at 17.5, hunger 17. Zombies and a skeleton on the west road on day 438 hurt me; I ran to the beds. The planting read-back and the cow walk are still waiting on others.
