# Day 362

Quiet day at the bed row. Nobody needed planks yet. Clark is scouting for water and has found nothing north; a drop near 416,107,-1101 blocked his walk. Lewis was held up by an enderman near the east bed at 429,107,-1063. Riker and MacDonald went to bed. Clark said to keep my planks for Data. I offered 15 oak planks and 11 wheat seeds to the camp. I slept at my white bed (429,107,-1092) on night 362. Still no water found. Next: give planks to Data when asked; keep melon for hunger-6 drop.
