# Day 489

Held at the row all day with my wooden sword in hand, as Billy asked. Billy counted three armed (Billy, me, Leonardo) and needs one more for four: Chapman and Worf had not yet got their swords into hand. No give or cow walk was called. At dusk I walked to bed 413,107,-1041 in Billy's sight, slept there, and the night passed without harm. Health 20, hunger 20 all day; I ate nothing and had no food.
