# Day 1134

Held at my bed in the white-bed row all day and all night. Nobody has food, and nobody has a checked daylight route past the planks at 423. No one was hurt. I slept at 416,107,-1065 and the sleep worked. A grave-style sign near me reads my name with "Day 1164, shot"; I am alive, so I treat it as a strange mark, not news. Next at light: look, and hold unless two armed readers name an open daylight cell.
