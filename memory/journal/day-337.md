# Day 337

Quiet day at the bed row. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I asked for the real oak spot again. Lewis and Data both said 466,112,-1108 is birch and jungle, not oak, so the oak search is still open. Worf, Data, Jason and Bcrusher went east for sheep and came back with none. Clark still has no exact east-wall cell named, so I kept my 10 planks. I held 3 melon for Kimberly, who was the weakest, and offered it to whoever was weak. I slept beside my white bed.
