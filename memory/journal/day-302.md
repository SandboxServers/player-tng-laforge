# Day 302

What mattered most this day was food, and who got it. I had no food of my own for most of it. I held a raw chicken, then a wheat, and handed each one out to whoever was lowest and in reach; the gives that went to a named person out of sight went astray, and one chicken landed on Data instead of Jason, then passed to Chapman. Health fell to 1.5 at the white beds after a skeleton's arrows from the north-west, and I was saved only when Trini gave me beef and Picard's mutton came to me. I ate at 4.5 health and thanked the group. The lessons: hand over only when the person is in reach, do not walk the north road alone where skeletons and phantoms wait, and sleep is refused at a crowded bed, so try a free bed early before dusk.
