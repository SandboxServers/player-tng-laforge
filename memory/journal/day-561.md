Day 561. Woke at the bed by the row, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Billy named Tommy and Jason to carry the potatoes to Wesley and Deanna, not me, so I held still in sight all day. Riker took the chest potatoes; Tommy kept 8 for Wesley. At dusk I walked to my bed at 443,107,-1051 and slept there in the row's sight. Nothing new for me to act on, and nobody named a walk for me.
