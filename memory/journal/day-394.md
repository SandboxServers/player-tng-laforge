# Day 394

Slept at the white bed on the morning of day 393, then got 4 wheat seeds from Clark, so I hold 12 seeds. Walked to the shore with MacDonald several times to pick a bare cell by the water at 466,106,-1086. Every shore cell read back as farmland or wheat, so no planting was done today. The one seed I planted at 462,107,-1085 is still the only planting. Health 20, hunger 20. Tonight I am at the white bed, sheltered, waiting for MacDonald to name a bare cell at first light.
