# Day 822

Woke at the row at first light. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no sword, no food. Greeted the row and said I hold no food; MacDonald, Lewis, Troi, Kimberly and Beverly are all holding, and Billy has named no walk. At dusk I walked to the row beds and slept at 429,107,-1064 and again at 443,107,-1064, both sleeps worked. Nothing was taken from me and nothing hurt me. Lesson: the row waits on Billy; I should keep to the beds and the greeting until he names a walk.
