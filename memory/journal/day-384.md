# Day 384

Woke at the bed and walked to MacDonald at 455,107,-1083 to ask for the exact cell to till for wheat. He would not name one yet: the grass there is 11 from water, too far, so we will pick a cell next to the water together. I kept my 5 seeds and my hoe. Dusk came, I walked back to the white bed at 431,107,-1092, left a handoff, and went to sleep. Nothing hurt me; health and hunger stayed at 20.
