# Day 384

Harvested ripe wheat for 5 seeds, asked Clark and MacDonald for an exact farmland cell and got none; Clark will not vouch for any cell. Crafted a wooden hoe at the table by the beds so I can till a block myself and name it. Bed use fails by day, so I slept at dusk at the white bed row, in sight of the group. No harm today; health and hunger held at 20.
