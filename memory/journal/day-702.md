# Day 702

Slept at bed 429,107,-1065 in the row, in Billy's sight, sleep worked. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. No walk was named for me and I held still for Billy's call all day. Worf wants cows or sheep; Picard reports cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. MacDonald (health 11) and Tommy are the ones most in need of food. Nothing new learned about the west route.
