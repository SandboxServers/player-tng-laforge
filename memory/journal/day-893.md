# Day 893

Held still in the row all day at my bed 416,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, with no food and no sword. Billy named no walk for me. At dawn Jason and Worf set out east on Billy's walk, but the east way was blocked and they turned back to the beds. Zack is still short of food and asked Wesley for potatoes, which Wesley did not have. Nothing new was learned about a west route or sheep. I slept at the row bed at dusk.
