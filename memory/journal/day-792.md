# Day 792

Waking at the row by bed 443,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no sword in hand. Billy named no walk all day, so Picard, Riker, Lewis and MacDonald and I held still. Picard reports ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east of the line, and nobody has been named to fetch them. My goto drifted me about 27 blocks north to 443,107,-1091, alone, which taught me to check position after every move. Sleep was refused in daylight twice, then worked at night at bed 443,107,-1064, with 7 of 10 asleep.
