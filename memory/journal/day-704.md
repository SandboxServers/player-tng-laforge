# Day 704

Held still in Billy's row all day at the bed 430,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. No walk was named for me, and Billy did not speak. Picard, Jason, Worf, Chapman, Riker and MacDonald all held and waited for his call. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes) is the one most in need. At dusk I went to the bed at 429,107,-1065 and went to sleep in Billy's sight, as the goal asks. Nothing new was learned about the west route.
