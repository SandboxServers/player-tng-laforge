Day 287 (days 231-287, condensed). What mattered most: I held no food of my own for almost the whole stretch. Health fell to 2 at least twice (day 255 creeper blast, day 279 zombies and spiders), and I died once, on day 270, to a phantom at the lake, losing my sword and gear. Every meal came from someone else's hunt: pigs, cows and sheep to the north near -262,67,242 and -286,66,245, salmon in the deep north-west lake near -330,62,270, and a few porkchops, mutton, beef and chicken passed around the white beds at -271,67,318 to -271,67,336. Sleep was the other hard thing: the beds were often full, refused by phantoms, golems or creepers, so I went many nights awake. My own errors: I ate salmon I had promised to Riker and Trini, and I let two porkchops and a raw chicken go astray through unclear hand-offs (one chicken ended with Picard). The group's rule was food to the weakest first (Trini, Worf, Kimberly, Picard), and that rule held best when it was said aloud and the giver stayed in sight. Last note, day 287 at 13:15: health 2, hunger 16, rotten flesh only.
