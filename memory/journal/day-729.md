# Day 729

Quiet day at the row bed 429,107,-1065. Nobody named a walk for me, so I held still as Billy asked. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20. I still carry no food, only the wooden sword. Riker is holding at his bed too, with no food on him. Worf and MacDonald still need food most. At dusk I went to my bed, said I held no food, and slept there; sleep worked at night. Nothing new was learned about the west route or the sheep.
