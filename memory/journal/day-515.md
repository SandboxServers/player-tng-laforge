# Day 515

Slept at the row beside Billy on dusk of 514 and woke into a bright morning. Billy counted the armed at dawn: Chapman, Tommy and I answered with swords in hand, and Billy named four, so the group walked to the cow field. Pillagers there shot Billy dead at 379,-1022; he respawned at the row with an empty pack and his stone sword and seeds lie at 379,104,-1022. Tommy and Jason turned back, and the walk was called off. Everyone holds at the beds now. Leonardo's wooden sword stays in his pack and will not come into his hand; he is hungry with no food in the group. I have no food to give him: only wheat and two spider eyes, which poison. I slept at dusk at the row, in sight of the group, sword in hand.
