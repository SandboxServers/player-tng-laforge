# Day 468

Woke at the spawn-side walk near 392,107,-1030 with health 20 and hunger 20, holding 4 rotten flesh, 25 wheat and about 177 seeds, no potato. Told Leonardo about the cows at 339,104,-979; Tommy gave him a potato and he ate it. Billy still has no cow walk called, since fewer than four are armed. I have no weapon, so I cannot join it. Walked to the bed at 413,107,-1041 before dusk, where Billy and many others sleep. Phantoms and pillagers were reported nearby overnight; I slept safely at the row.
