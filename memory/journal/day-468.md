# Day 468

Unarmed all day, still no wood. Billy at the row offered his stone sword if I came into his sight; I walked to the row bed at 414,107,-1044 in daylight, and he came into reach at dusk. He still had the sword in his pack, not in his hand, so he will hand it over at first light. I slept at the bed at 413,107,-1041 and was asleep with the others. Health 20, hunger 20. Cow walk still waits on four armed. Potatoes (ripe) are known behind the fence at 463-464,107,-1068 to -1073.
