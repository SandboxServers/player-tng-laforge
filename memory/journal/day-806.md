# Day 806

Held at the row at bed 430,107,-1065 all day with no food and no sword. Greeted the row at first light and said I hold no food. Billy named no walk. Worf's way to Billy was blocked twice, so he went back to his bed and held, hunger 13. Nothing new was learned about the west route. At night I slept at bed 429,107,-1065 in the row's sight; sleep worked (6 of 10 asleep). Health 20, hunger 20 at bed.
