# Day 317

Planks went out most of the day: Data got 9 (Riker carried her 9 too), Clark got 9 and 8 and 6 from me across the night, and I kept 5 for Lewis. Lewis was weak (health 6) and said he was in sight, but I could never see him, so the give failed twice. Hunger stayed full, but many around the camp have no food: Troi, Riker, Lewis. Lesson: give only when I can see the person, and the same goes for handing out food. I slept at the white beds. Next: at first light, hand Lewis the 5 planks and the melon slices, then help find chickens or melons.
