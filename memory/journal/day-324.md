# Day 324

Went east to find oak for Data. The trees at 460,108-112,-1120 were jungle logs, not oak, so the errand is still open. Offered melon aloud; no one took it. Came back at dusk, said goodnight to Clark, who still offers melon and planks in the morning, and slept in the white bed by the beds at 443,107,-1092. Health 20, hunger 20, holding 3 melon slices. Nothing hurt me today.
