# Day 324

Quiet day at the beds. Health and hunger stayed full, and no one called for melon. Data's oak logs handover stalled all day because the east route kept getting walled. I went east to 459,109,-1113, the spot Clark named, and found jungle logs at 460,108-112,-1120, not oak. Lesson: check the log kind before a trip. I came back to the beds and slept in the white bed at dusk. Still holding 3 melon for anyone weak.
