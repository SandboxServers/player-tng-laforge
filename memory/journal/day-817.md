# Day 817

Held still at the row all day beside bed 430,107,-1065 with Riker in reach. Greeted the row in the morning and said I hold no food and no sword. Billy named no walk, so I did not walk. Heard Jason and Worf try the way east and find it blocked; Beverly is low on health and Billy is keeping the potato for the worst off. At night I slept beside bed 429,107,-1065. Still unarmed and foodless; health 20, hunger 20.
