# Day 894

Slept at the row bed 416,107,-1065 through the night, woke at daylight with health 20, hunger 20, no food, no sword. Billy led a daylight walk to Worf with his one potato, which was blocked and handed over at the bed. I was not named on any walk, so I held still at my bed all day. Riker reached the row at health 11, hunger 17. Billy slept at 413,107,-1041 at dusk. I slept again at the row bed at dusk. Nothing new was learned about west or east routes.
