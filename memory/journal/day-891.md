# Day 891

Quiet day at the row. Health 20, hunger 20 most of the time, pack empty, still unarmed and with no food. Billy named no walk, so I held at my bed 416,107,-1065 with the row in sight. Zack and Jason asked for a walk to the cows at 364,-1010; I declined, since it is west and unnamed. Worf, Beverly and Jason still need food; I have none to give. Slept at my row bed at night, in sight of Billy and the row. Nothing new was verified about the west route.
