Day 456. Called at the beds at dawn; no one came. Walked to the water cell in daylight and along the east side of the potato fence, out to about 474,107,-1070. No gate or gap found; the fence runs solid on every side I saw, with ripe potatoes and beetroots inside. The walk back was blocked once and I went around. Back at the beds before dusk, said I had food to share, and slept safely. Nobody from camp has answered for many days.
