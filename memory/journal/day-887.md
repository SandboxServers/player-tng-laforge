# Day 887

Held still at my bed 416,107,-1065 all day in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, no sword, pack empty. Billy named no walk for me, so I did not walk west or fetch the pile. Worf and Zack want food, and Beverly is at health 7; I have none to give. Slept at dusk at the same bed; the sleep verb worked, but the night ended with me still awake by the bed. Nothing new learned except that the row is still waiting on Billy.
