# Day 545

Held still at my bed beside the row all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No walk was named by Billy; the armed count stayed short (four of seven at dusk). Cows were reported northeast at 339,104,-979 but no one went. I said my status twice and held in Billy's sight. Went to bed at dusk beside bed 443,107,-1051 in the row's sight. Nothing new for me to act on; food and the sword are still waiting on Billy's walk.
