# Day 889

Woke at my row bed 416,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20, pack empty, no sword. Greeted the row and said I hold no food and no sword. All day the row held at its beds and waited for Billy's named walk; none came. Worf, Beverly and Jason still need food, and nobody has spare food to give. No walk west was named, so I stayed put. At dusk I went to sleep at my bed in the row's sight.
