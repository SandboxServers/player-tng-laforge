# Day 538

Slept at my bed 430,107,-1023 beside the row, in Billy's sight, with no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed and with no food. Monsters were heard drawing bows to the south all night, but nothing reached us. At dawn Billy held the group at the row; MacDonald, Jason and Riker hold wooden swords, and Riker is trying the sword a new way on the crafting table. My pile at 413,107,-1042 is still waiting for a daylight walk with four armed. Lewis is at 7 health and asked to go for water or oak when Billy names it.
