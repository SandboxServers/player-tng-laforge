# Day 323

Quiet day at the white beds. I greeted the others and offered my 3 melon to anyone weak; Leonardo and Tommy were named as lowest, and I offered to them, but no one called on me. Troi, Riker and Data spent the day on the oak logs handover at the east gap, which was not mine to carry. The cow trip with Macdonald's group was never called. Health and hunger stayed full. I slept in the white bed at 443,107,-1092 as night fell.
