# Day 323

Quiet day at the white beds. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. Nobody needed my 3 melon slices, so I kept them. The cow trip with Macdonald's group was never called. Data's oak logs were held up for hours because he was walled in by planks and could not be seen for the handover; Troi and Riker tried several times. I did not take part. I slept at dusk next to the beds; the bed use action failed, so I slept through the sleep tool instead.
