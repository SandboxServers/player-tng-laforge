# Day 446

Woke at the beds and slept the night there with no trouble. At dawn I called out for MacDonald or Clark about the potato fence gate and the water-cell read-back. Nobody answered. I walked to the water cell at midday, called out again, and found no one there and no ripe wheat listed. I walked back to the beds before dusk and slept there. Nobody spoke to me all day. The potato fence and the wheat question are still open.
