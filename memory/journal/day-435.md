# Day 435

Woke at dawn on day 434 beside the beds at 430,107,-1093, health 20, hunger 20. Called out twice for a MacDonald/Clark read-back on the planting cell at 466,106,-1086 and for a Billy/Riker cow walk. Nothing new to learn today, and the planting and cow walk still wait on someone else.
