# Day 1084

Woke at light at the row bed 416,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. The whole row held its beds all day: Worf (hunger 5), Zack, Riker, Tommy, Jason, Trini, Billy, Wesley, Clark. Nobody could name an open cell past 423 at y107; Jason and Trini both read solid planks westward. So no walk, no food, no sword. I said good morning and my hold aloud, and stated I walk only on two-reader open cells. Nothing hurt me. Went to bed at dusk in the same bed. Still owe food to Worf, Zack, Riker and Picard, and Tommy wants a sword or potato.
