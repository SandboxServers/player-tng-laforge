# Day 436

Quiet day at the beds at 430,107,-1093. I slept through the night of day 435, woke at first light, and called out twice for a MacDonald or Clark planting read-back on the water cell 466,106,-1086, and for a Billy or Riker cow walk. No one answered all day. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I hold 4 bread, 2 wheat and 124 seeds. Nothing new was learned. Going to bed again beside the beds; the sleep was partway (4 of 10 asleep) at the close.
