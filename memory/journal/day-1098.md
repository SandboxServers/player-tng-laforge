# Day 1098

Held at bed 416,107,-1065 all day with an empty pack, health 20 and hunger 20. The row stayed in a stalemate: Trini held two potatoes at bed 430 and would not walk past the planks at 423 without two armed readers naming open cells, and nobody named any. Worf (hunger 5) and Zack were first in line for food, then Riker, Tommy and William. I could give nothing and named no route. I slept in my bed at dusk with no errand running and no walk taken.
