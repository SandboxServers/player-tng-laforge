# Day 627

Held at my bed 416,107,-1065 in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. No walk was named for me. Picard and Tommy tried the west walk on Billy's word; the way was blocked at 416,107,-1053, so they stopped short and the walk was called off at dusk. I said my morning and dusk lines, then slept at my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing new learned about the west oak planks.
