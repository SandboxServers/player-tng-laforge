# Day 399

Woke hungry at 19 with no food. Raw wheat would not eat ("no food"), but I could craft bread from my 3 wheat at the table at 433,107,-1089, and bread filled me. Walked to the farm shore at 462,106,-1087 to find MacDonald for planting. Called out once. Nobody came all day, so no planting. At dusk I went to the bed at 431,107,-1092 and slept, with 2 of 10 needed asleep when the call returned. Nothing was dropped and no one was hurt.
