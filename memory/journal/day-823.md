# Day 823

Quiet day in the row. I woke at night beside bed 429,107,-1064, health 20, hunger 20, with an empty pack and no sword. Billy named no walk, so I held still and did not go west or south. Picard, Jason and Lewis each said goodnight and held their beds. I said goodnight too, and then slept in the bed beside me. Nothing new was learned about routes west. Still needed: food for Beverly and Riker, and a sword. Next: at first light, greet the row, say I hold no food, and wait for Billy's word.
