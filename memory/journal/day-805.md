# Day 805

Quiet day at bed 430,107,-1065. Greeted the row and said I hold no food and no sword. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave the row. Lewis holds a potato for Chapman and rotten flesh for Riker, who is at health 6 and hunger 17; Worf is at hunger 13 and owed a potato. Slept beside bed 429,107,-1065 at dusk, in the row's sight. Still unarmed and without food; the dropped pile at 413,107,-1042 is untouched.
