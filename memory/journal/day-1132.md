# Day 1132

Held at the row bed all day with no walk and no death. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, still unarmed. Nobody has food, and no open daylight cell past the planks at 423 has been named. Worf said good night and that he and Zack and Jason sleep near the bed. Slept at the bed at night at 416,107,-1065 with nobody hurt.
