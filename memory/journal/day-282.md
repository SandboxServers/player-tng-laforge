# Day 282

Health 2 and hunger 16 all day with no food. Sleep at the white bed was refused overnight by a creeper, then at dawn I was told it was not night. Rotten flesh went to Trini (hunger 6, then 10) and Data gave one too; Picard was still waiting at hunger 5 at last report. Nobody found animals or ripe wheat; the group is planting farmland near -273,65,332. I stayed in reach of the group and did not wander with health 2. At dusk I slept in the yellow bed at -263,67,353 and 3 of 10 were asleep. Still no food on me. Next: give any meat to Trini, then Picard, then me.
