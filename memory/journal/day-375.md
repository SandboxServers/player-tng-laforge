# Day 375

Quiet day at the camp. I gave MacDonald the 13 wheat seeds he asked for, which worked. He then asked for the exact bare farmland block, and I could not name it from where I stood, so I asked Clark to point him to 462-463,107. Nobody called Billy's cow walk, so I did not join it. I walked back to the white beds at dusk, wrote a handoff note, and slept at 429,107,-1092 with health 20 and hunger 20.
