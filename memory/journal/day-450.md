# Day 450

Quiet day at the beds (430,107,-1093). Called for camp twice, once at dusk and once more before bed; no one came or answered. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Holding 6 potatoes, 25 wheat, about 170 wheat seeds, 1 red mushroom, 4 rotten flesh. Slept in the bed at dusk, nothing new. The potato patch (462-468,107,-1072/-1075) is still walled and the gate is still unknown.
