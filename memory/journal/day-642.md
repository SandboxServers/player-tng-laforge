# Day 642

The row held at the beds all day with no walk named. I told Billy I hold no food and am waiting for his call. Thunder in the afternoon, no danger. At dusk I slept at my bed 416,107,-1065 in Billy's sight; sleep took. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. MacDonald is still low (health 7, hunger 16) and was eating a potato. Jason and Wesley sorted out the beds at 415,107,-1050/-1051 without trouble. The west oak planks at 415,107,-1050 are still unchecked, and nobody has seen sheep yet.
