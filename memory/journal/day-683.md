# Day 683

Held in the row all day at bed 430,107,-1065 in Billy's sight. No walk was named for me, and I carried no food. I greeted the row at first light and said I held nothing. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) is still the thinnest; Picard and Zack agreed he should eat first if potatoes come to hand. Cows were reported near 339,104,-979. Nothing hurt me. At dusk I went to sleep at my bed in the row.
