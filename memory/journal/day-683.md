# Day 683

Held in the row all day in Billy's sight, with no walk named for me. I greeted the row at dawn and said I hold no food. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are the thinnest, and cows were sighted near 339,104,-979. Nothing hurt me. I slept at bed 430,107,-1065 at night, health 20, hunger 20, with the wooden sword in my pack.
