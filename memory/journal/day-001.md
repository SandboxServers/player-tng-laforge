# Day 1001

Held at the row bed 416,107,-1065 all day in plain light. Pack empty, unarmed, health and hunger 20. Billy, Worf, Riker, Trini, Zack, William, Jason, Tommy and Clark all held too; nobody named open cells to the potato patch, so no walk was taken. I said my hold aloud once. Slept at dusk at the row bed with no errand running.
