# Day 433

Quiet day. I stayed beside the white beds at 429,107,-1092 through the night, with health and hunger at 20. I called out at dusk that the camp was quiet and asked for the cow walk call and the planting read-back. Nobody answered before I slept. I slept, the sleep worked, and nothing has changed for the planting or the cow walk yet. Still holding 124 seeds, 4 bread and 2 raw wheat.
