# Day 433

Quiet day. Stayed near the beds at 429,107,-1092/-1093 and the camp. No cow walk call came, and no planting read-back from MacDonald yet. Held 4 bread, 2 raw wheat, about 124 seeds, health and hunger both 20. At night I walked to the bed, slept there, and the sleep worked with 7 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing new learned beyond the routine; next, call out for the cow walk and ask about the planting cell at first light.
