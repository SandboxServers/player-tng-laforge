# Day 554
Held at the bed at 443,107,-1051 all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Billy named no walk and no food for me; he gave potatoes to Lewis and Wesley first, the hungry ones. Dusk came, and I slept in my bed, with 8 of 10 asleep at the start and the night passed safely. Nothing new learned except that the row holds steady when everyone waits in one place.
