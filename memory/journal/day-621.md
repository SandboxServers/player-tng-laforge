# Day 621

Quiet day in the row. I held still at bed 416,107,-1065 with my wooden sword in pack, health 20, hunger 20. Billy had named no walk. Worf and MacDonald also settled for the night and called out that they were holding. At dusk I slept in my bed in Billy's sight and woke safe. Nothing new about the west route: the oak planks at 415,107,-1050 still block it. Still no food in my pack.
