# Day 621

Held at my bed 416,107,-1065 in the row all day. Billy's daylight call sent Tommy with a spare sword toward Wesley, but the west path was blocked again at 413,107,-1050 and Tommy turned back. No west walk is named for me. Nothing hurt me. I have no food yet; hunger is 20. I told the row I have seen no sheep. At dusk I said goodnight and slept at my bed. Still curious about the oak planks west of Wesley's bed at 415,107,-1050.
