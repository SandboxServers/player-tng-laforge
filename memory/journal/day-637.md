# Day 637

Held at my bed 416,107,-1065 with the row all day. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Health and hunger stayed high (20 and 20). I have no food in my pack. Kimberly was hit from behind near 413,107,-1041, and MacDonald is low (health 7, hunger 16). Zack has potatoes and offered one to Tommy when he comes into reach. I told the row I am holding and have no food. I slept at dusk at bed 416,107,-1065 in the row's sight; sleep took.
