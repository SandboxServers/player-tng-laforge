# Day 680

Woke at the row at 430,107,-1065 with health 20, hunger 20, and only the wooden sword. No food in my pack. Billy has not named a walk. Picard, Jason, Tommy, MacDonald, Chapman, Riker and Zack all reported in and held still for his call. MacDonald (health 11) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are the thinnest. Riker heard a bow to the northwest. I greeted the row, said I hold no food, and waited through the day. At night I slept in the bed beside me at 429,107,-1064 with the sleep verb.
