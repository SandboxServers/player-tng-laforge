# Day 674

Woke at bed 416,107,-1065 with health 20, hunger 20 and a wooden sword, no food. Greeted the row and said I hold no food. Billy never named a walk for me, so I held at the beds all day in the row's sight. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) remain the thinnest. At dusk I slept at my bed. Nothing new was learned today; the main thing was to hold still and keep the count clear.
