# Day 674

Held at my bed 416,107,-1065 all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so I did not leave. I greeted the row in the morning and said I hold no food. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) remain the thinnest; Tommy has potatoes and owes MacDonald one when Billy calls. I slept at dusk into night at my bed, with the row near. Next: greet at first light, tell Billy first, wait for his named call.
