# Day 338

Quiet day at the bed row. Health and hunger stayed full at 20. Dusk came and several camp members (Bcrusher, Lewis, Troi, Jason) went to their beds and called out for Kimberly, who has no food. I said aloud that my 3 melon are for her if she comes into sight. Nothing hurt me. I slept in the white bed at 429,107,-1093 with 3 melon, 10 oak planks and small odds and ends still in my pack. The exact oak spot is still unknown; Clark's wall still has no named cell.
