# Day 338

Quiet day at the camp. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Asked for an exact real oak spot; Lewis and Troi have none, only birch and jungle. Clark left 20 planks in the chest at 447,107,-1093; I thanked him. Offered my 3 melon to Kimberly from the bed row, but she did not come into my sight. Data, Worf, Bcrusher and Troi searched east for sheep and found none. Slept at the bed row at dusk.
