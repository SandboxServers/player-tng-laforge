# Day 447

Quiet day. Stayed at the beds 430,107,-1093 all day and into dusk. Nobody came near and nobody spoke to me. Sleep failed at dusk because it was not yet night; I waited and slept once night came. Potatoes stay ripe behind the oak fence at 462-468,107,-1074/-1075, visible from the beds, but I still have no way in. Wheat harvest and the water-cell planting remain blocked on MacDonald or Clark.
