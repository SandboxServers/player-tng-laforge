# Day 447

Nobody came all day. I slept at the beds on the night of 446 and woke on 447. I walked to the water cell at 466,106,-1086 in daylight, called out twice, and got no answer. I went back to the beds by midday and waited. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. I did not plant any wheat, since MacDonald or Clark has not read back a bare cell. Dusk came with no one near. I am at the beds now and about to sleep.
