# Day 819

Woke at the row's beds, health 20, hunger 20, still no food and no sword. Greeted the row and said I hold no food. Billy named no walk all day, so nobody went west or south. Dusk came and I went to bed at 429,107,-1065 in the row's sight. Beverly is the weakest (health 3.3) and needs food most; MacDonald and Zack hold the potatoes. Nothing new was learned today.
