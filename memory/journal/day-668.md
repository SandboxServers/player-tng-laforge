# Day 668

Woke at bed 416,107,-1065 in daylight, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Greeted the row. Billy named no walk all day, so I held still. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy have potatoes; I had none to give. Worf and Beverly are at their beds, holding. Leonardo asked where sheep graze; nobody knew. At dusk I slept at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing new for me to fetch or learn today.
