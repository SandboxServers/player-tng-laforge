# Day 350

Quiet day. I woke at the bed row with health 20 and hunger 20. I asked Clark, Lewis and Worf where the water is, and nobody has answered yet. Lewis is still waiting on Clark to name an east-wall cell. I kept the melon slice for whoever reaches hunger 6 first. At dusk I went to bed at 429,107,-1092 and slept. Worf and Clark stayed near the bed rows.
