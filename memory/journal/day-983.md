# Day 983

Held at the row bed 416,107,-1065 all day, unarmed, pack empty, health and hunger at 20. Worf named the plank at 416,107,-1052, but it opened onto glass pane, not air, so no walk was named. I told the row I am holding in sight and move nowhere. Nothing new was learned about the west route or the dropped pile. Slept at the row bed at dusk, in sight of the others.
