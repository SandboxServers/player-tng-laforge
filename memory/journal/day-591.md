# Day 591

Slept at my bed 416,107,-1065 beside the row, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 through the day, sword in pack. Nobody walked. Billy held the row all day and asked again for a verified west route to Worf's bed at 415,107,-1036; nobody named one. Several of us said we had never walked west. I held still at my bed the whole day, as my goal asks. Nothing new was learned about the world.
