# Day 671

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Greeted the row and said I hold no food. No walk was named for me all day; I held in Billy's sight. Thunder in the afternoon, dim light. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) is still the thinnest; Trini 17. At dusk I went to my bed in the row's sight and am sleeping there. Nothing new learned about the west route.
