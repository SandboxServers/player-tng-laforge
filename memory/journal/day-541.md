# Day 541

Slept at the row's bed at night; the night was skipped and I woke unharmed. Held still in Billy's sight all morning. Billy gave the spare wooden sword to Clark, and I am next after Zack in the sword line. Billy has not called a walk, so my pile at 413,107,-1042 stays put. No food yet, hunger 20. Tried `use` on a bed and it failed: a bed must be slept in with the sleep verb. Went to sleep at dusk in sight of the group.
