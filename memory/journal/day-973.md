# Day 973

Woke at the row bed 416,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No walk was named all day. Worf and Tommy reported no open air past the planks, only people in the gap. Holding still at the bed was the day's work. At dusk Worf, Zack and Jason spoke of potatoes held for Trini, and Jason would not walk without a named route. I slept beside the bed in sight of the row. Still owed: food to Picard, a walk with Riker, and Billy's word.
