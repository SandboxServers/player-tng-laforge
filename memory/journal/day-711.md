# Day 711

Held in the row at the bed 430,107,-1065 all day, in sight of the others. Greeted the row at first light and told Billy I hold no food, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. No walk was named for me, so I stayed still. Worf, Riker and John hold for Billy's walk; Tommy has potatoes; MacDonald (health 11) needs food most. Wesley's bed at 416,107,-1050 was blocked and he drifted to 418,107,-1047; Jason holds near 415,107,-1051. At night I slept at the bed beside me, in the row's sight.
