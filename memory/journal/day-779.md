# Day 779

Held still at the row all day with no walk named by Billy. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Picard and Zack went back to their beds to hold with Billy; John (hunger 17, health 11) and Worf (14) still need food, and Lewis keeps his potato for Chapman. No route west verified. At night I slept beside the bed at 443,107,-1051 in sight of the row. Nothing new learned except that the row is still waiting.
