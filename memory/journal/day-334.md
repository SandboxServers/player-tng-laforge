# Day 334

Morning at the bed row, health 20, hunger 20, holding 3 melon for anyone weak. The wall work stayed paused: nobody named an exact open cell, so I kept my 10 planks. Data planned a northern sheep search but did not find us in sight, so it did not go ahead. Lewis reported ripe wheat near 463,107,-1087 and good birch and jungle wood past the birch ground. At night I tried the bed at 429,107,-1092 by use and it refused; sleep worked from where I stood and the night was skipped. Nothing hurt me today.
