# Day 670

Woke at the bed 416,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. The row held at its beds all day and no walk was named for me. I greeted the row, said I hold no food, and waited in Billy's sight. Nothing dangerous came. At dusk I went to sleep at my bed in the row's sight. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) is still the thinnest. Sheep are still unseen; only Wesley's cows are known.
