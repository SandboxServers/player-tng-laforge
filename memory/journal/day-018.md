# Day 1018

Held at the row bed 416,107,-1065 all day in sight of the others. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nobody named an open cell past 423 at y107; my own view showed only planks. No walk was taken. Billy and Tommy checked the row chests for a weapon and found nothing usable. Worf is at hunger 8 and Zack at 14; I still owe them food and have none. Slept at the bed at night.
