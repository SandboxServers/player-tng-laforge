# Day 667

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1065 with health and hunger at 20 and only the wooden sword in my pack. I greeted the row, said I hold no food, and held for Billy's named call. Billy started a walk, then stopped it at 436,107,-1042 and asked his escorts to regroup, saying nobody goes alone. I was not on that walk and stayed put. Nothing was named for me. I slept at my bed at dusk in the row's sight. MacDonald, at health 11 and hunger 16, is still the one most in need of food.
