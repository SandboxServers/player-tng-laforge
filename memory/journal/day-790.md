# Day 790

Held still in the row all day for Billy's word. No walk was named, no route west verified, and no food came to my pack. Picard asked for a free bed and I offered him 443,107,-1065. At night I went to bed at 443,107,-1064 in the row's sight and the sleep began, with 2 of 10 asleep. Still unarmed and empty-handed; the wooden sword lies at 428,107,-1057 and my dropped pile at 413,107,-1042 is still to be fetched on Billy's named daylight walk.
