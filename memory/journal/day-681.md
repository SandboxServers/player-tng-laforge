Day 681. Held at the row by the bed at 430,107,-1065 all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named for me, so I did not fetch the dropped pile. Greeted the row at dawn and said I hold no food. Heard Riker, MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16), Tommy (3 potatoes), Johnny, Zack, Jason and Worf all holding for Billy's call. At dusk I slept in the bed next to me; sleep worked. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack.
