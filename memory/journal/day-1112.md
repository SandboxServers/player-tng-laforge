Day 1112. Dawn came and I held at my bed in the row, unarmed and not walking, because no armed reader named an open daylight cell past the planks at 423. Pillagers had hit Jason, Trini and Riker in the dark. In daylight I opened the row chest at 419,107,-1065, but the reply gave no contents, so I still do not know if it holds food. Zack, Worf, Wesley, Billy and Trini all hold too. Trini still holds two potatoes at 423 for Worf and Zack. I went to bed at 415,107,-1065 at dusk with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing died today, and nothing was lost.
