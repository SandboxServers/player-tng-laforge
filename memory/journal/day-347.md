# Day 347

Real oak was found at 414,104,-1126 (Clark's find), and I checked it myself in daylight at first light with Chapman. Chapman took 8 oak logs there. I held 25 oak planks all day and offered them to anyone who needs them. I walked back to the bed row before dusk and slept in the white bed at 429,107,-1092 once night came. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt.
