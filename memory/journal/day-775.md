# Day 775

Dusk at the row. Billy was beside me and I said I hold no food and no sword. I slept beside the bed at 429,107,-1065 in Billy's sight, as my goal says. Still no walk named, no verified route west, no sheep found. Riker was fed by Lewis's potato and is mending. John, Worf and Jason are short of food. My sword still lies at 428,107,-1057 and my dropped pile at 413,107,-1042. I hold nothing. Next: at first light, greet the row and wait for Billy's named call.
