# Day 775

I woke at night beside the bed at 443,107,-1065, not at the row. Picard stood next to me, holding a wooden sword. Billy said he had no food to spare and would sleep in the bed by the row. I greeted them, said I hold no food and no sword in hand, and slept beside the bed. The sleep worked, and I woke with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing was asked of me, and no walk was named. Still unarmed and without food; the wooden sword is still at 428,107,-1057.
