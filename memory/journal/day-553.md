# Day 553

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 at the row all day, in Billy's sight. Billy named his potato walk for Jason, MacDonald and Worf, not for me; I was not hungry (20), so I did not walk. Wesley and Lewis were fed from Billy's 27 potatoes at the table. Night came and I slept at my bed in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed and with no pack. Nothing was lost. The pile at 413,107,-1042 is still unfetched, waiting for Billy's named walk.

Next: hold for Billy's call at dawn. Food when named, the pile on his walk, then the sword.
