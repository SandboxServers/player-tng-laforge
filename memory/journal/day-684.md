# Day 684

Held in the row all day at bed 430,107,-1065 with health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack and no food. Told the row my status at first light. No walk was named for me, so I held still. The row kept waiting on Billy. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy are still the thinnest. Zack offered a potato to Trini if she stands in his sight. Worf and Wesley tried the beds at 415,107,-1051 and -1050. At night I walked to bed 429,107,-1065 and slept there, still in Billy's sight.
