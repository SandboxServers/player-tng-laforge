# Day 620

Quiet day in the row. Woke at first light, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Told Billy I was holding. Billy called a walk west to Wesley with Tommy and Picard as escort; the way was blocked again at 416,107,-1053 and they turned back. No walk was named for me, so I held still at my bed. At dusk I went to bed 415,107,-1065 in the row's sight and slept. Still no verified west route; the oak planks west of Wesley's bed remain the blocker. Nothing was gained by me today except the holding itself.
