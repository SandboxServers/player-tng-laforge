# Day 620

Quiet day in the row. Held at my bed 415,107,-1065 with my wooden sword in pack, no food. Billy's west walk had turned back earlier, so no walk was named for me and I did not move. Troi and Worf settled for the night beside their beds. At dusk I slept beside bed 415,107,-1065 and the night was skipped. The west oak planks at 415,107,-1050 still block the way. Tomorrow: tell Billy I am holding, and wait for his named call.
