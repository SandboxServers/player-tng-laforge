# Day 978

Quiet day at the row bed 416,107,-1065. Health and hunger stayed at 20 from waking to dusk, and the pack stayed empty. Nobody named a walk, and Worf held at the bed while he waited for daylight to name a route to the north-west through the gap, with his stone sword and Jason and Zack in sight. Riker, Tommy, Wesley, Clark and Jason all held still and wait for a named daylight walk. Worf said he would look for food for Leonardo and Picard first. I did no errand and walked nowhere. I said goodnight at dusk and slept at the row bed, in the row's sight. Still unarmed and without food; Picard's food is still owed.
