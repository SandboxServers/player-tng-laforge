# Day 613

Held at my bed 416,107,-1065 all day in Billy's row's sight, sword in pack. Billy named no walk, so nobody moved. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Heard the row report in each morning and dusk. Billy asked to be called at hunger 10. Wesley asked about his bed at dusk and was told to hold until first light. At dusk I slept at my own bed in sight of Billy.
