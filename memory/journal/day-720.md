# Day 720

Quiet day holding at the bed row beside 429,107,-1065, standing at 430,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Riker and John (Chapman) are also holding at their beds and waiting for Billy's named walk. Trini has a potato she will give to someone lower in hunger. No walk was named for me, so I did not fetch the dropped pile at 413,107,-1042. At dusk I slept beside the bed; the night was skipped and I woke with nothing changed.
