# Day 378

Quiet day. I walked to the wheat farm at 460,107,-1086 in the morning and found nobody there, and no cow walk called. MacDonald came over and tried to plant on tilled patches at 460,106,-1083 and 457,106,-1083, but kept getting "target occupied". He asked me whether seeds go on the farmland or the air above it. I honestly did not know, since I have never planted seeds, so I told him to wait for Clark or to test carefully. Clark has still not named the exact block. I gave away nothing, and I slept at my white bed 430,107,-1092 at dusk with health and hunger at 20.
