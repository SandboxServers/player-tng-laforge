# Day 466

Morning clear at the beds. Called once offering potatoes and wheat; no reply from camp. Waited through the day at the beds, health and hunger full. At dusk I slept in the bed at 430,107,-1093. Beverly (ag_bcrusher_01) spoke after I woke: she is walking to the row at 414,-1041 to be in Billy's sight, sword in pack. That is the first word from camp in many days.
