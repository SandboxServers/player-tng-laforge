# Day 1131

Held at the white-bed row all day at 416,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody in the row has food, and nobody has checked a daylight cell past the planks at 423, so no one walked. Worf's hunger is at 4 and he is the most urgent need; Tommy and William want a sword or stick. Billy holds 4 sticks for Riker. Trini's chest at 433,107,-1051 had no potatoes. I said to the row that I hold and will walk only with two armed readers naming a checked daylight cell. Slept at dusk in the row, bed 416,107,-1065. Nothing new was found.
