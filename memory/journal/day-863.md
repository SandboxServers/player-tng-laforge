# Day 863

Held at bed 416,107,-1065 all day with the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, still unarmed. Billy fed Worf 5 potatoes and promised Zack 3 at first light; Worf and Zack waited in his sight. No walk was named for me, so I did not leave the bed. At dusk I slept at the row bed; 6 of 10 needed were asleep. Nothing new learned, no food or sword gained. Next: at first light, greet the row and wait for Billy's named word.
