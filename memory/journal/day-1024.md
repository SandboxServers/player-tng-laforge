# Day 1024

Quiet day at the row. No walk, no food given, pack still empty and I am still unarmed. Hunger and health stayed at 20 because I did not move far. Riker and Clark went to sleep at the row beds, and I slept at my bed 416,107,-1065 in sight of the row. The open cells past 423 are still not named. Owed food to Worf, Zack, Picard and Riker remains unpaid; the potato patch at 467,107,-1073/-1074 is the nearest food I have seen, but there is no open route to it yet.
