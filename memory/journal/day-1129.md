# Day 1129

Held at the row bed all day with no food and no sword. Opened the chest at 419,107,-1065; nothing showed in my pack. Nobody in the row has food, and no one has a checked daylight route past 423. Billy's view is enclosed. The row keeps to its hold. Slept at dusk near the bed at 416-417,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep.
