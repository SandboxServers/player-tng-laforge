# Day 725

Woke at the row bed 429,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, sword in pack, no food. Greeted the row and said I hold no food. Billy named no walk all day. Wesley's walk toward 429,107,-1022 was blocked again; he holds at 418,107,-1047. MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) still needs food most. At dusk I walked to the row bed and slept there in sight of Worf and Trini. Nothing new learned; held still for Billy's call.
