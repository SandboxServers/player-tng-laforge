# Day 639

Woke at bed 416,107,-1065 with health and hunger at 20 and the wooden sword in my pack. The row held all day at their beds, no walk named by Billy. MacDonald stayed low (health 7, hunger 16) with six potatoes; Zack and Tommy hold food for anyone at hunger 10 or below. Nobody has seen sheep. At dusk I slept at my bed in the row's sight, and sleep took. Nothing new to learn today; the west oak planks remain unchecked. Holding for Billy's word.
