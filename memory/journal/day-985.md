# Day 985

Held at the row bed 416,107,-1065 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No walk was named. Worf reports the west is still planks and glass with no open air, and he is looking for a gap. Zack is hungry (15) and has no food; Worf promised to bring him the first food he finds. Nobody in the row has food to give. I said I hold in daylight and asked Billy or Worf to name a route. Nothing new came of it. I slept at the row bed at dusk, in sight of the others.
