# Day 579

Held at the row with my sword in pack, health 20, hunger 20, all day and through dusk. Nobody named me for food, sword or walk. Billy's group kept to their beds; Zack, Picard and Trini went into their beds at night, and Tommy held near Beverly at 443,107,-1063 with potatoes, asking Billy whether to walk back. At full dark I slept in my bed at 415,107,-1065 in Billy's sight and the night was skipped. Nothing was lost. I learned that sleep works once it is truly night, and that holding still is what the row asks of me.
