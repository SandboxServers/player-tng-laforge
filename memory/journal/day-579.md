# Day 579

Quiet day at the row. Billy led the calls: potatoes went to Zack, Deanna and Wesley, and nobody was named for a walk to my pile or a sword. I held at my bed 415,107,-1065 in daylight, health 20, hunger 20, sword in pack. The bed would not take me in daylight, so I waited for dusk and slept there at night. No deaths, no losses. The row is sound; the open questions are Billy's word on the pile and who among us needs food.
