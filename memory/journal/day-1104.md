# Day 1104

Held in the row again, at bed 415,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No plank past 423 was named open by any reader, so I broke nothing and walked nowhere. Tommy is awake with no food or sword and asked for a potato or wooden sword; I had neither to give. Sleep only partly took (5 of 10 asleep) and I was not fully rested. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt.
