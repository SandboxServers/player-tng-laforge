# Day 381

Quiet day at the white bed. No seeds yet, so I could not help MacDonald plant. Farmland was still not named by anyone with coordinates; Clark would not name a cell and pointed back to MacDonald. Billy still has not called the cow walk. I said goodnight at the bed, offered my 15 oak planks to any builder, and noted Clark is hungry (health 11, hunger 13) with 3 melon in hand. Slept at the white bed at night.
