# Day 547

Woke at the row beside my bed at 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Billy never named a walk, so I held still in his sight all day, as my goals say. Clark, Zack, Worf, Tommy and Wesley held too. Food is still the gap: the potatoes behind the fence and the cows at 339,104,-979 are not yet reached. At dusk I slept at my bed; the first attempt was partial, the second worked. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt.
