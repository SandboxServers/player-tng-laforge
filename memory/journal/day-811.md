# Day 811

Held at the row all day with no walk named by Billy, pack empty, no sword, health 20 and hunger 20. Greeted the row at first light and said I hold no food. Heard Jason, Clark, Worf, Lewis and Zack all holding too; Zack keeps 2 potatoes for Worf, Lewis keeps rotten flesh for Riker. At dusk I stood beside bed 429,107,-1065 and slept; 7 of 10 were asleep and I woke with nothing changed. Nothing new was learned, and no route west was verified.
