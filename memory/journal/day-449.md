# Day 449

Woke at the beds 429-430,107,-1092/-1093, health 20, hunger 20. Called for MacDonald, Clark, Billy and Riker in the morning, twice, and no one came. Nobody spoke to me or came near all day. I stayed at the beds in daylight, did not walk at night, and slept at dusk. Still holding 6 potatoes, 25 wheat, about 170 wheat seeds, 1 red mushroom and 4 rotten flesh. The camp feels quiet; I have had no answers for three days.
