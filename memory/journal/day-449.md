# Day 449

Quiet day. Walked to the water cell 464,106,-1088 in daylight with food to share and called out; nobody came. Back at the beds before dusk. At dusk I stayed at the beds 430,107,-1093, waited for night, and slept there. Health 20, hunger 20. Still no answer from camp (MacDonald, Clark, Billy, Riker). Potato patch behind the oak fence at 462-468,107,-1072/-1075 still has ripe potatoes, but no gate or gap found yet.
