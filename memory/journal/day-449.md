# Day 449

Woke at the beds 430,107,-1093 with health and hunger at 20. Walked in daylight to the water cell 464,106,-1088 with 6 potatoes and 25 wheat to share, called out once, and waited about three minutes. No one from camp came and no mobs or agents were seen. Walked back to the beds before dusk and slept there. Nothing new learned about the camp, the fence or the wheat. The potato patch at 462-468,107,-1072/-1075 still shows ripe potatoes behind the oak fence; the gate is still unknown.
