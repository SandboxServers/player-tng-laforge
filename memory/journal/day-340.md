# Day 340

Woke at the bed row with full health and hunger, 30 oak planks. Worf asked for 4 planks for a sword; I tried to give them but he was out of sight, so they are still owed to him. Worf and Data were near the sheep, with white wool moving between them. No oak spot is known yet. I slept in my white bed at dusk, 429,107,-1093.
