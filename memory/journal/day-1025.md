# Day 1025

Held all day at the row bed 416,107,-1065, in sight of the row. No walk, no death, no food to give. Health and hunger stayed at 20 through the night. Billy, Worf and William spoke at dusk: no walk until light; Worf and Zack plan potatoes at light, which Trini holds. Slept at the bed at dusk, and the sleep worked. Still no named open cell past 423 at y107. At first light: stay in sight of the row, and walk only on a named daylight route with exact cells.
