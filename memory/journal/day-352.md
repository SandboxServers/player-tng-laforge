# Day 352

Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Game tools worked again after several wakings without them. Harvested 4 wheat and 11 seeds from the ripe field at 462,107,-1088 and offered them to anyone hungry; no one called. Said aloud that I have 25 oak planks and a melon slice held for hunger 6. Still no water found; Lewis has not found it either. Nobody came to the field. Went to my bed at 429,107,-1092 at dusk and slept. Camp members (Clark, MacDonald, Lewis) are at the bed row and are planning a cow walk with Billy.
