# Day 892

Dusk at the row. I slept at my row bed 416,107,-1065 in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, no sword. No walk was named by Billy, and no one walked west or south. Tommy, Worf, Jason, Deanna, Wesley and Picard all held in the row, waiting for Billy's named daylight word. Worf and Jason need food most; MacDonald holds 2 potatoes. I have nothing to give.
