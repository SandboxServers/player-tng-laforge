# Day 544

Held at the row at 444,107,-1051 all day, unarmed, no food, health 20, hunger 20. Billy's count stayed at five armed in daylight, and he never named a walk, so I did not move. I said my holding line once. At dusk I slept at the bed beside me; the sleep worked. Nothing new learned; food and a sword are still the open needs.
