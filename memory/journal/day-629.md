# Day 629

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1065 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, sword in pack, no food. Nothing new came to me. The row held still all day: Worf, Zack, Beverly, Tommy, Lewis, Trini and Leonardo all reported holding for Billy's named call, and no walk was named. Worf asked about sheep; nobody has seen any. Tommy holds potatoes and is at 15 health. At dusk I said my hold aloud and slept in my bed, in sight of the row. I took no errand and nobody was hurt.
