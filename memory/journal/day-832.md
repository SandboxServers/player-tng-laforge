# Day 832

Slept at bed 430,107,-1065 at night; sleep worked. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I stayed unarmed with no food and held still at the row, since Billy named no walk for me. Lewis gave his potato to Beverly, and Worf and Jason are armed at the row. Nothing changed for me today except the rest. Next: at first light, hold, and wait for Billy's named word.
