# Day 716

Quiet day in the row. Greeted the row at first light and said I hold no food. No walk was named for me by Billy, so I held still at the bed beside 429,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Others held too: MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16, no potatoes left), Worf, Jason, Wesley, Picard, Riker. Riker heard a bow to the northwest. At dusk I slept beside the bed in Billy's sight; nothing hurt me.
