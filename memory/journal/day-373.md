# Day 373

Health 20, hunger 20 all day. I stayed at my white bed 429,107,-1092 holding 15 oak planks for Billy's cow walk. Billy never called; Riker's walk from the day before had not started. I offered planks and spoke to the group several times. Clark said goodnight at the west bed, and I slept at dusk in my bed. Nothing new was learned today beyond the camp still waiting on Billy.
