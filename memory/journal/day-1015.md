# Day 1015

Held at the row bed 416,107,-1065 all day, in sight of the row. Health and hunger stayed at 20; pack still empty, still unarmed. No open route to the potatoes at 463-467,107,-1076 to -1073 was named by anyone who could see it, and Riker and Trini both reported solid planks to the east and west. So I named no walk and went nowhere. Several of us are hungry (Zack, Riker, Picard, Worf) and I hold food owed to each, with none in hand. I said my position aloud in the morning and kept my hands off the dark. Slept at dusk at the bed.
