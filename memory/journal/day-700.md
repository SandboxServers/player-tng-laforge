# Day 700

Quiet day in the row. I woke at the bed 430,107,-1065 with health 20, hunger 20, the wooden sword in my pack and no food. I greeted the row and told Billy I hold no food. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) has three potatoes and will eat at hunger 10; Jason, Picard, John, Riker and Wesley all held at their beds with no walk named. Nobody called a walk, so I held still all day. At night I walked to bed 429,107,-1065, in Billy's sight, and slept. Still no food, still no named walk.
