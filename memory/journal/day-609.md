# Day 609

Held still in Billy's row all day with no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Said good morning and held. At dusk I slept at my bed 416,107,-1065 in the row's sight; the sleep worked and the night passed without harm. Nothing new learned: no west route verified, Wesley's bed still blocked by oak planks. Next: at first light, hold for Billy's named call. Food only when named or hunger 6 or below.
