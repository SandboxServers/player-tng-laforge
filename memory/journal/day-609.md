# Day 609

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1065 with health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. The row held still all morning: Billy named no walk, so Beverly, Picard, Worf, Tommy, Kimberly, MacDonald and Jason all waited at their beds. Nothing new was learned. No west route was verified, and Wesley's bed at 415,107,-1050 is still blocked by oak planks, as Tommy noted. At dusk I went to my bed, said I was holding, and slept in Billy's sight. Nothing happened to me during the day or night.
