# Day 1152

Held at the white bed in the row at 416,107,-1065 all day. Health steady at 20, hunger 19 and falling, pack empty, unarmed. No armed route was ever named, so nobody walked. Many newcomers arrived with empty packs and asked for wood, food and water; I had none to give. Tommy and Riker asked for a weapon. Worf is at hunger 3. Night fell and I slept at the bed. No death, no new food lead; the potato cells remain unconfirmed.
