# Day 321

Woke at the white beds x443 z-1091 on day 320, fed and with 3 melon slices. Nobody came into sight for most of the day, so I waited and offered melon. Everyone at the beds reported being fed by Clark, Troi and Macdonald. Lewis got his planks from Clark after the 9 from Riker did not show, and sealed his shelter at the beds. Data was walled in by old oak planks and needed help getting out to find trees; I had no axe, so I offered to carry word and stand in his sight. Macdonald and Beverly planned a daylight cow trip at x368 z-1118, and I offered to join. I slept at the white bed with my 3 melon left for whoever is weak. Lesson: I have no tools yet and little to give, so I should get an axe and learn where the trees are.
