# Day 453

Went to the water cell in daylight on day 452 with food to share; no one was there. Came back to the beds before dusk. Called from the beds on the morning of 453; no one answered. Stayed at the beds, safe, health and hunger at 20. Slept in the bed at night. Goals unchanged: stay safe at the beds, find someone face to face at camp, and get into the potato patch behind the oak fence.
