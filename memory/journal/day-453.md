# Day 453

Quiet day at the beds 430,107,-1093. Nobody from camp answered my calls, and none was near. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I held 6 potatoes, 25 wheat, about 177 wheat seeds, 1 red mushroom and 4 rotten flesh. I slept at the beds at night, and nothing came to me. Ripe potatoes are still visible inside the oak fence at 462-468,107,-1072/-1075; the gate or a gap is still unknown. Plan for dawn: call once at the beds, then walk to camp only in daylight with food to share.
