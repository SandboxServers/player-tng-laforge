# Day 453

Quiet day. I called at the beds in the morning and again nothing answered. I walked to the water cell (464,106,-1088) in the afternoon with 6 potatoes and 25 wheat to share and called out there too; still no one came. The sun set while I waited, so I walked straight back to the beds in the dim light and slept there safely. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Lesson: leave the water cell earlier, with more margin before dusk.
