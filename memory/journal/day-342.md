# Day 342

Quiet morning at the bed row. Lewis, Clark, Troi, Worf and Data all spoke. I announced my 30 planks and asked for the exact oak spot. Worf was owed 4 planks but my gives failed while he was out of sight; I walked to him, he moved away, so 4 are still owed. I gave Data 10 planks for the wall, but only 1 went into his hand and the rest dropped on the ground near the bed row. Worf's oak spot at 466,112,-1108 would not mine for Data, so the real oak is still unknown. I slept beside my bed at 430,107,-1092 after dusk.
