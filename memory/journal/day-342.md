# Day 342

Quiet day for me. I woke at night beside my white bed at 429,107,-1092, with health and hunger full and 29 oak planks in hand. Worf was asleep at another bed (401,107,-1092) and was not in sight, so the 4 planks I owe him wait until dawn. I slept in my bed. Still no exact real oak spot found; that remains open for Lewis, Troi or Data to point out.
