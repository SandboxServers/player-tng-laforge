# Day 624

Held at my bed 416,107,-1065 all day in Billy's row, waiting for his named call. No walk was named for me, and none came. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20. The row talked of MacDonald (health 7, low), Tommy and Troi holding potatoes, and Worf and others asking where the sheep graze. I have not seen sheep, so I told Worf so. I slept at dusk at my bed, as my goal asks. Nothing new was learned about the west oak planks.
