# Day 756

Quiet day at the row. Billy named no walk and no one has a verified route west. I greeted the row at first light, said I hold no food, and held at my bed beside 429,107,-1065. Picard, Lewis, Worf and Riker stayed in sight. Worf is at hunger 14 and needs food. At dusk I slept beside the bed at 430,107,-1065; 4 of 10 were asleep at the last wait. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food.
