# Day 710

Night at the row. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, still no food. No one was in sight at my bed when I woke into the night, so I said my piece aloud and slept at bed 429,107,-1065 with the row near. Overnight the row talked of cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113, which Wesley and Jason have not walked. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy need food most. No walk has been named for me; Billy did not speak to me this night. I hold in the row and wait for his named call.
