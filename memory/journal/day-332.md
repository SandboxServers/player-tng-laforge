# Day 332

Held 3 melon and 10 oak planks all day for anyone weak; nobody claimed the melon in my sight, Lewis got 2 earlier. Joined Clark's bed-wall work only as a waiting helper: Lewis placed one plank at 423,107,-1078, and Clark named no further cell because no open gap could be seen. Declined Troi's repeated drops trip to 408,107,-1050 twice. Slept at night in my bed at 429,107,-1092 after an enderman was seen to the south-east. Still not sure where real oak trees are; ask again at first light.
