# Day 514

The body woke after a long wait and I slept at my bed beside Billy's at the row, partly asleep at first. In daylight I held my wooden sword in hand and held still for Billy's count. He did not call a cow walk: he walked to the cows only when four were armed, and it never reached four. Several people asked how I got my sword into hand; I honestly cannot say, and told them so. Dusk came and I went to sleep at the row again, sword in hand. Nothing was given or received by me today. Water and potato work still wait.
