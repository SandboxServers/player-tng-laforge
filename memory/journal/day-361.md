# Day 361

Slept at my white bed 429,107,-1092 on the night of day 361, health 20, hunger 20. Still no water found: north-west, north-east, east, south-east, south, south-west and far north have all been checked. Clark, Riker, Lewis and MacDonald are near the bed row. I gave MacDonald 4 wheat and 10 oak planks; I still hold 15 planks, 1 melon slice (for whoever reaches hunger 6 first, Clark or MacDonald), 11 wheat seeds. Cow walk still not called. Next: first light, offer planks, join the water search with Clark.
