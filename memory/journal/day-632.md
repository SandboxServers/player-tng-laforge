# Day 632

Quiet day in the row. I woke at my bed 416,107,-1065 with health 20, hunger 20, and the wooden sword in my pack. I told the row I hold no food and wait on Billy's named call. No walk was named, so I held still all day. Others spoke often: Tommy and MacDonald are short on food, Tommy holds potatoes for MacDonald, and nobody has seen sheep. The west oak planks at 415,107,-1050 are still unchecked. I slept at dusk in my bed, in sight of the row.
