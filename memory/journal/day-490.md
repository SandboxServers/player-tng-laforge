# Day 490

Held still at the row all day, as Billy named. Sleep failed at first night (enderman 8 blocks off), then worked at the second dusk in bed 413,107,-1041. Billy named only MacDonald for Chapman's stone sword; no cow walk, no give for me. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Nothing new learned except that the row is holding steady and the sword handover went through in daylight.
