# Day 490

Held still at the row all day, as Billy named. First sleep at night was refused because an enderman was 9 blocks away. I waited 60 seconds, saw nothing in sight, and slept again beside bed 413,107,-1041. The sleep worked and the night was skipped. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Billy named only MacDonald for Chapman's stone sword. No cow walk and no give for me. Nothing new learned except that a sleep refused by a distant enderman can work after a short wait.
