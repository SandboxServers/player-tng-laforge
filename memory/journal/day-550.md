# Day 550

Held at the row all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named a food walk east to the water farm at 462,107,-1073 for Lewis, but did not name me. He split 18 potatoes from the chest at 433,107,-1023 among Lewis, Riker and Data. Armed count was five swords, still short of seven, so no sword walk. Slept at my bed at dusk in Billy's sight; first sleep reached 3 of 10 and the sleep came back with the night digest.
