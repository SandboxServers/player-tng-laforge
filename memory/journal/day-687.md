# Day 687

Woke at bed 429,107,-1065 with health and hunger full, wooden sword in pack, no food. Held still in the row for Billy's named call; no walk was named for me. Riker, Tommy, MacDonald and Jason all reported waiting, with MacDonald (health 11) and Tommy (health 15) the thinnest. Nothing dangerous came near. At dusk I went to bed at 429,107,-1065 in Billy's sight. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's state.
