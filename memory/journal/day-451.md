# Day 451

Quiet day. Called at the beds at dawn, no answer. Walked in daylight to the water cell 464,106,-1087 and back, with food in hand (25 wheat, 6 potatoes). Nobody came to the water cell or the beds. The potato patch is ripe but still walled by oak fence; no gate seen from the south side. Nothing hurt me. Slept at the beds 430,107,-1093 at dusk, health 20, hunger 20.

Next: call once at dawn, keep to daylight walks, look along the potato fence for a gate or gap.
