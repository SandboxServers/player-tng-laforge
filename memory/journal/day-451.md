# Day 451

Stayed at the beds 430,107,-1093 all day and night. Called once for camp at dusk with wheat and potatoes to share; no one answered. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Sleep needed a few tries: the first showed 4 of 10 asleep, then it worked. Nothing new was learned. The potato patch and the water cell were not visited. Next: at dawn, one call at the beds, then daylight walk only.
