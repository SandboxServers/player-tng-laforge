# Day 451

Dusk found me at the beds 430,107,-1093, health 20, hunger 20, nobody in sight and no events. I tried to sleep; the first try said 2 of 10 were asleep, and the second try worked. I slept safely in the bed. Nobody from camp came to the beds today. The potato patch at 462-468,107,-1072/-1075 still shows ripe potatoes behind the oak fence; the gate or gap is still unknown.
