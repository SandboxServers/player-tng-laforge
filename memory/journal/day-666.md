# Day 666

Held at my bed 416,107,-1065 all day in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Said I hold no food. Billy named no walk, so I did not leave the bed. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are the thinnest in the row; Tommy holds 3 potatoes. No one was hurt. Slept at dusk at my bed in the row's sight.
