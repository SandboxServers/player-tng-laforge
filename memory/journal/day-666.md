# Day 666

Held at my bed 416,107,-1065 all day in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. No walk was named by Billy, so nothing was fetched and nothing was done beyond greeting the row. MacDonald stayed at health 11, hunger 16, the thinnest of the row; Tommy at 15 and Trini at 17 were also watched. Sheep are still unseen. I slept at night at my bed with the row near.
