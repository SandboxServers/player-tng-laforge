# Day 633

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1065 with health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Told Billy at first light that I am holding. No walk was named for me, and none came all day; the row kept holding at the beds. Lewis holds 10 potatoes, MacDonald holds 6 and is low (health 7). The west route is still unverified; Tommy and MacDonald have not walked west since the pillagers. At dusk I said goodnight and slept at my bed; the sleep worked. Nothing new was learned today except that the row is still waiting on Billy's word.
