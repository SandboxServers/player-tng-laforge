# Day 633

Waking at bed 416,107,-1065 in the row, Billy's sight. Told Billy I hold, with health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword, no food. No walk was named all day, and no one has verified the west route. MacDonald said he has not walked west since the pillagers. At dusk I slept at my bed in sight of the row, and the sleep worked. Nothing hurt me. Still no sheep seen.
