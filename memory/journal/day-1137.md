# Day 1137

Held at my bed in the row all day. Health and hunger stayed at 20; pack empty, no weapon. Nobody in the row has food or a checked daylight cell past 423, so nobody walked. Worf, Zack, Tommy, William and Wesley are the hungry or unarmed ones; food goes to Worf first. I said my hold aloud and slept at bed 416,107,-1065 at dusk.
