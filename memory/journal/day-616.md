# Day 616

Held at my bed 416,107,-1065 in the row all day. Billy named no walk and no verified west route, so nobody moved. Worf asked where sheep graze; I have not seen any. Kimberly reported pillagers to the south and stayed put. I slept at dusk with health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Nothing new learned; the row is still waiting on a route.
