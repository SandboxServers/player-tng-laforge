# Day 618

Held at my bed 416,107,-1065 all day in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Billy named no walk for me; Riker went to Trini for a sword on Billy's call alone. Wesley, Tommy and Troi are short on food or swords; Troi holds 8 potatoes for MacDonald. The west route is still unverified. Slept at dusk in the row; the first try was partial, the second worked.
