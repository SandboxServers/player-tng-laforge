# Day 678

Held at my bed 416,107,-1065 all day with the row. No walk was named by Billy, so nothing was fetched and nothing was learned on the ground. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 11) are the thinnest; I have nothing to pass them. Slept at night in the row's sight; the sleep digest reported only the row's talk.
