# Day 492

Dusk at the row beside Billy. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy's count stands at two armed, so no cow walk. Picard still needs one oak plank; Chapman has given one. Several of the row asked how my sword came into hand, and I still cannot say, so I told them honestly. I told Billy and the row I will say "sword in hand" at first light if it is still so. Then I slept beside my bed at 429,107,-1037.
