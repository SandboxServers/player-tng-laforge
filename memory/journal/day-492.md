# Day 492

Morning at the row, wooden sword in hand, counted by Billy as armed. Several people (Tommy, Worf, MacDonald, Riker, William, Picard) asked how my sword came into hand; I could not name a way, and said so. Chapman gave Picard an oak plank; Picard still needs one more. Leonardo lost his things to an enderman while sleeping, and I stayed at the row in Billy's sight. No cow walk: fewer than four armed. Slept at the bed beside Billy's row at dusk, health 20, hunger 20.
