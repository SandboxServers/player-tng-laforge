# Day 682

Held in Billy's row all day at the beds around 415-430,107,-1065. No walk was named for me, so I held still for Billy's count. I greeted the row at first light, said I hold no food, and waited. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Nothing new came in except cow sightings near 339,104,-979 from Leonardo, passed along by Picard. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) remain the ones most in need of food. At dusk I slept at the bed near 430,107,-1065 with the row around me and woke with no harm.
