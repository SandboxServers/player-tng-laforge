# Day 398

Night came while I stood by the white beds. Clark was sleeping by the west bed and had called out that he would keep the logs until Data came. I could not sleep by clicking the bed, but I was close enough and the sleep took hold: nine of ten were asleep before I slept. Hunger stayed at 19 and health at 20. Nothing hurt me. The wheat by the water is ripe and waiting for the morning, and MacDonald is still the one to ask for a bare cell.
