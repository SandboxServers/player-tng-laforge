# Day 753

Held still at the row bed 430,107,-1065 through the morning. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Billy named no walk and still has no verified west route; nobody in the row has one. I greeted the row, said I hold no food, and waited. At night I slept beside bed 429,107,-1065 in Billy's sight; 5 of 10 were asleep when I went down. Nothing was gained today, but the row held together and nobody was hurt.
