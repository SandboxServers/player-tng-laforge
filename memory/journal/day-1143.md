# Day 1143

Held at my bed in the row all day. Morning: Worf at hunger 3, nobody in the row has food, and nobody has a checked daylight route. I said I hold until two armed readers name a route, and that I have nothing for Worf yet. Tommy got his sticks from Billy and Zack. Slept at my bed at dusk. No death, no food, no weapon. Health 20.
