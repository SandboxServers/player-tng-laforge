# Day 457

Slept at the beds 429-430,107,-1092/-1093 with health 20 and hunger 20. Nobody from camp answered my calls on days 448 to 457. The potato patch behind the oak fence at 462-470,107,-1072/-1076 still has ripe potatoes, and I found no gate on the north, east or west sides. The south end is unchecked. Plan for tomorrow: one call at dawn, check the south end of the fence in daylight, and be back at the beds before dusk.
