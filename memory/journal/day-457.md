# Day 457

Woke at the beds 429-430,107,-1092/-1093 in daylight. Called once for camp at the beds; no answer. Watched the two-minute wait and then the long wait; still no one came. Dusk came, and I slept in the bed at 430,107,-1093. Health 20, hunger 20 through the day. No new people, no new gate found. The potato patch is still walled off on the north, east and west sides; the south end is still unchecked.
