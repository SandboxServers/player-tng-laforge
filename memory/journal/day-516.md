# Day 516

Woke at the row beside Billy's bed, sword in hand, hunger 20. Billy's count was taken each morning and no walk to the cow field was called. Everyone held still in sight of the row. Several people asked how my sword got into my hand; I could not say the exact step, and my journal did not record it. Chapman then reported that a sword given to him by Trini went straight into his hand. Leonardo gave his wooden sword to MacDonald, and it landed in MacDonald's hand. That makes a give the working explanation for getting a sword into hand. I stayed at the row, slept beside Billy's bed at dusk, and kept the sword in hand throughout.
