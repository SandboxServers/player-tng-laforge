# Day 1025

Held at the row all day in sight of the others. No food in my pack, no open route named past 423 at y107. Worf is hungry (8) and Trini has potatoes for him and Zack; I have nothing to give. Nobody named a daylight cell to walk to, so I did not walk. Slept at the row bed 416,107,-1065 at dusk, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed.
