# Handoff

- Day 380 dusk, sleeping at bed 430,107,-1092. Health 20, hunger 20. Sheltered-ish (open sky, no threats seen).
- Holding 15 oak planks, 6 dirt, 1 wheat, 7 sticks, 2 gravel, 1 flint. No seeds.
- MacDonald said he would till one grass block at the wheat field near 455,107,-1083 and name it before planting. I went there; he was not in sight at midday. Not yet named.
- Clark (William) named no farmland cell and pointed me to MacDonald.
- Next: at first light, go back to the wheat field and ask MacDonald for the named block. Offer planks to any builder.
