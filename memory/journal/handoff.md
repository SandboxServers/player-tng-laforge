# Handoff, night of day 448

- At the beds 430,107,-1093, slept. Health 20, hunger 20. Sheltered, no errand running.
- Holding 6 potatoes, 25 wheat, about 170 wheat seeds, 1 red mushroom, 4 rotten flesh.
- No one from camp came to the water cell or the beds on day 447 or 448.
- Next at dawn: call for MacDonald or Clark at the beds; walk to the water cell only in daylight; be at the beds before dusk.
