# Handoff

- Morning 1116, at 417,107,-1065 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Row chest at 419,107,-1065 opened in daylight; no contents came back. Read as empty.
- Row holding with no food. Jason health 5. Trini holds potatoes at 423. No open cell past the planks at 423 named by two armed readers.
- Next: at dusk or full night, go to bed at 416,107,-1065 and sleep. At light, look, then hold unless two armed readers name a daylight route.
