# Handoff, dusk of day 446

- At the beds 430,107,-1093. Health 20, hunger 20. Sheltered, no errand running.
- Holding 6 potatoes, 25 wheat, about 170 wheat seeds, 1 red mushroom, 4 rotten flesh.
- Called out for MacDonald/Clark twice (at beds and at the water cell). No one came.
- Ripe wheat was not listed nearby at midday; potatoes still ripe behind the fence at 462-466,107,-1072/-1075.
- Next at dawn: call for MacDonald/Clark again; walk to water cell in daylight if still no one; be at the beds before dusk.
