# Day 448

Woke at the beds 430,107,-1093 on day 447 with health and hunger at 20. Walked to the water cell 466,106,-1086 in daylight and waited there; no one from camp came. Walked back to the beds before dusk and waited. Still no one from MacDonald or Clark. Deep water lies one step south of the water cell, so I stayed off that side. Slept at the beds at night. Nothing new was learned about the potato fence, wheat harvest, or the cow walk. Next: call for MacDonald or Clark at dawn; go to the water cell only in daylight.
