# Day 600

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1065 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Every neighbour held still and named no walk: Tommy, Jason, Picard, Beverly, Troi, John, Kimberly. Billy gave no call all day, so I held. No west route was walked and nothing was learned new. At dusk I slept at my bed in Billy's sight, as my goal says. Nothing hurt me and nothing was lost.
