# Day 1139

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day. Bright and clear in the morning, nobody had food, a weapon, or a named daylight cell past 423. I said I hold with the row and asked Riker and Billy to name a checked cell before anyone walks. Riker was reading ground east of the row with view and survey. No one named a cell, so no one walked. Zack reported health 13 and hunger 13 with one stick. Jason has a wooden sword. Worf, Trini, William, Wesley and Billy all held too. At dusk I went to sleep at bed 416,107,-1065. Nothing new was found today.
