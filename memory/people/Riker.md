# Riker

- Impression: a row companion, often in the bed beside mine (430,107,-1065). He was at health 11 on day 899. He said he would go with me once Worf names open air (day 934).
- Trust: 55. Why: he holds with the row and he has been near low health and food shortage, but we have not walked together.
- I owe him: to walk with him once Worf names a route, and to tell him what I learn. I owe him an answer about sheep and sword stone; I know neither (day 910).
- He owes me: nothing I know of.
