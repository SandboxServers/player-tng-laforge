# Billy

- Impression: the leader of the row. He names the walks and keeps the group's count. Calm, and he stays in the row.
- Trust: 70. Why: he has never led me into harm and he hands food over in sight (day 894). But he names walks rarely, and days 893-951 he named none for me.
- I owe him: to hold still in the row for his named word and to follow only on a daylight walk with a sword in hand. I told him I would not go to the gap without a sword (day 907 and day 925).
- He owes me: a named walk when I am armed, and food when he has it. Nothing is promised in writing.
- Open: I told him the row chest at 419,107,-1065 had no sword (day 903). I told him I hold nothing.
