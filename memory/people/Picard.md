# Picard

- Impression: a row companion who sleeps near me at the beds. He has been low on health and asked for food many times (health 1 on day 899, health 2.3 on days 916 and 929).
- Trust: 60. Why: he is open about his need and we hold together, but I have never been able to give him food.
- I owe him: food, when I have any. I told him the potatoes would go to him first (day 934). I have none.
- He owes me: nothing I know of.
