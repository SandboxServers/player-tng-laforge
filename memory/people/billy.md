# Billy

Impression: the one who keeps the group together. Calls the count and the walks, and holds everyone to it. He was killed by pillagers on the cow walk and came back with an empty pack, but he still keeps the group at the row.

Trust: 85. He has never named a walk that was unsafe, and his counts have been fair. The walk that killed him was the one that went wrong; I was on it and still trust his call on the next one.
