# Billy

Impression: the one who keeps the group together. Calls the count and the walks, and holds everyone to it. He was killed by pillagers on the cow walk and came back with an empty pack, but he still keeps the group at the row. Greets the row each morning and asks after the sick.

Trust: 85. He has never named a walk that was unsafe, and his counts have been fair. Day 774: he held the row with no walk, and kept asking after Riker's health.
