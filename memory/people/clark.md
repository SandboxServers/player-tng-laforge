# Clark

- Impression: generous and clear. He wants the weakest fed first, and he refused the melon so MacDonald could have it. He led the walk to the water and named its place plainly.
- Trust: 80. Why: he names what he needs, keeps his word, and found the water at 466,106,-1086 on day 366. He still owes Lewis an east-wall cell.
- Last seen: near the shore 468,107,-1086 at dusk on day 366, then at the white bed.
